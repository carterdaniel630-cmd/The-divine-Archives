## Phase 1 — summary of findings (full detail in `phase-1-audit.md`)

- **Competitive:** 6 sites surveyed (Britannica religion, Theoi, World History
  Encyclopedia, Sacred Texts Archive, Mythopedia, Godchecker) + 3 narrower tools.
  The field splits into *warehouses* (wide, unframed) and *boutiques* (deep, myth-only,
  culture-siloed). **No incumbent bridges all four of:** chronological + cross-cultural,
  full coverage (lived religion/doctrine/esoterica/modern, not just antique myth),
  a uniform **evidence-honesty apparatus**, and a modern non-paywalled UX. That
  intersection is empty and is exactly where Divine Archives is aimed.
- **Content gaps:** headline traditions per era are well covered; **8 clear P1
  must-adds** (Canaanite/Ugaritic, Jainism, Manichaeism, Hermeticism, Renaissance
  ceremonial magic, Bahá'í, Golden Dawn/Thelema, global Pentecostalism), ~20 P2
  traditions/variants, ~10 high-value **comparative-theme** chapters (we have 1 of
  a mandated one-per-era), and ~4 mandated **symbology-theme** chapters with no
  current home. Archive could ~double while every add still clears the sourcing bar.
- **3 distinctiveness moves:** (1) the three-lens **evidence engine as the site
  spine**, made uniform/comparable; (2) the **Motif Constellation** — browse by
  cross-tradition pattern with evidence-rated links (flagship feature); (3) the
  **Nine Ages synchronic timeline**. Plus (4) a **Symbol Lexicon** that doubles as
  the merch backbone.
