# The Divine Archives — Master Outline

A comparative reference on world religion, mythology, and the esoteric,
organized **chronologically by era**, not by region. Each chapter treats one
culture/tradition within its era, or one cross-cutting comparative theme
(flood myths, creation cosmogonies, and the like). One comparative-theme
chapter per era where useful, alongside the culture-by-culture chapters.

`ch01-the-flood.md` is the style template every chapter matches: concrete
opening scene/artifact, narrative body, explicit evidence-honesty close.

## Per-chapter content checklist (from CLAUDE.md)

Each tradition chapter should cover, where the evidence allows:

- **Textual record** — the primary texts, their dating and transmission
- **Cosmology & deities** — the shape of the divine world
- **Law & moral code** — what the tradition asked of people
- **Ritual & scribal practice** — how it was actually done and recorded
- **Archaeological evidence** — what the ground shows
- **Cross-disciplinary angle** — psychology / sociology / biology where relevant
- **Connections** — links to adjacent traditions in the same era
- **Evidence-honesty close** — supported / not supported / genuinely open
