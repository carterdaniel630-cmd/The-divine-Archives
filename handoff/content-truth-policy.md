# Content Truth Policy — The Divine Archives
**Binding editorial policy. Ordered by Carter, 2026-10-13.**

## The principle
The archive presents what was actually said, believed, and made — accurately,
completely, down to the letter. It is not about political correctness. It is
about history: understanding exactly what was said and why.

## What this requires
- **Philosophers' words are preserved verbatim.** Do not soften, paraphrase
  away, or omit difficult passages to make them comfortable. The research
  already caught misattributed quotes — the same rigor applies against
  sanitization.
- **Religions and faiths get the same treatment.** Present each tradition as
  its adherents understand it, with its real texts, real claims, and real
  edges intact.
- **No blurring, no backing off, no pulling back.** Gray censor splotches,
  bowdlerized quotes, and quietly dropped passages are banned. If something
  is in the historical record, it is in the archive — or its omission is
  explicitly labeled and justified, not silently blurred.
- **The creator's rights stand alongside respect.** Freedom of expression and
  Carter's rights as a creator matter. Respect for belief systems does not
  mean surrendering the truth of the record.

## The one exception
Pull back ONLY where content explicitly goes against a religion's own tenets
within its own sacred space — e.g., placing something the faith itself
prohibits inside that faith's room as if it belonged there. This is about
not violating the tradition on its own terms, not about shielding visitors
from discomfort.

## Why
"We're not going to be able to give the full truth if we're pulling back."
A museum that blurs its own exhibits is a brochure, not an archive.

## Enforcement
- Part of the Protection Program (ready line item 1).
- The High Council's sourcing auditor checks content integrity on every review:
  quotes verified against sources, no silent omissions, no blur artifacts.
- Applies to: Vault entries, Pantheon entries (including the 108 philosophers),
  museum text panels, video scripts, and all future content.
