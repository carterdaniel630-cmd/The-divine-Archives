## What the rebuild means
1. ANIMATED AND ALIVE — not merely prettier boards. Pieces move; the game
   plays inside its era's world, not on a bare diagram.
2. THE STORY STAGED IN AND AROUND PLAY — each game's real, attested story
   (its peoples, its board's history, what the record actually holds)
   staged in and around the play. TRUTH GUARD, absolute: story comes from
   the record, never invented fiction; where the record runs out, the
   game says so, the way the current packs' own text already does.
3. RULES AND CONTENT UNCHANGED — the games' rules, facts, and honesty
   marks carry over exactly. This order changes the grade of the build,
   not what the games teach or claim.
4. THE STANDARD governs: the highest possible within Muse's machine
   capabilities, Claude's capabilities, the system as built, and Carter's
   Galaxy phone — a piece is only as good as it looks and runs on that
   phone. Shiva remains the design floor; the Cinematic Floor (staged to
   the loop with Saturday's batch) governs presentation.
5. Ship-shape unchanged: each game stays a single file that opens in any
   browser — phone, tablet, computer — with no internet, no install, no
   account. That is the live products' standing claim and it must remain
   true of every rebuilt file.

## After the rebuild
A rebuilt game that passes its checks (playable end to end, rules intact,
story sourced, phone-grade performance) is handed back to atlas; atlas
swaps the new file into the live Gumroad product. The swap itself is
atlas's desk work, not this note's. Until a game's rebuild passes, the
current file stays live in the product and the product stays honestly
listed. Campaign pieces for the packs stay held until the swaps land.

## Acceptance
Per game: opens clean in a browser at phone size; a full game plays
through without error; movement and scene animation present (a static
board with new paint fails this order); every story element traceable to
the record; rules identical to the current live file. Carter judges the
result on his phone walk, as with every visual piece.
