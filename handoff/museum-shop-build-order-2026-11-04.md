## What the shop must never become
- No ads on the page, ever — the site's permanent law; a shelf is not an
  ad slot.
- No payment taken on the site — no cart, no checkout; the page shows and
  points, Gumroad sells and delivers.
- Nothing listed that is not live and verdicted; forthcoming items hold
  marked shelf space only, with no link.
- No pressure language — no countdowns, no scarcity claims. The house
  voice: wonder first, honest always.
