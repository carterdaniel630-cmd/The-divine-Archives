## OQ-1 — The Keeper's Table: ANSWERED (design settled)

Carter's words: "it's worked into the study. his space that only him
and i see with a performative copy of his desk where people can engage
the way we spoke about before."

The ruling: the Keeper's REAL desk stands in the Study — his private
space, seen only by the Keeper and Carter. What the public engages is
a PERFORMATIVE COPY of his desk, carrying the Table mechanics as
designed 2026-10-30: three works-in-progress on display, visitors
browse, watch clips, play-test, and leave reviews on the three prompts
(Did it hold you? What confused you? Should this be built — yes / no /
not yet?). Reviews remain evidence; Carter still decides.

This amends the 2026-10-30 table design, which placed the worktable
itself in the open museum: the open piece is the copy; the true desk
never leaves the Study. For the pilot: if the build reaches the Table,
it builds the performative copy. The Study interior is not a visitor
build, in VR or anywhere.

## OQ-3 — Pilgrimage sites in VR: YES

The pilgrimage sites become walkable VR places in their own right, in
their own phase; and the Living Sites inhabitants (era-true peoples)
are embodied there. All standing Living Sites standards apply

## OQ-4 — Pending-review rooms in VR: CURTAINED

Carter's words: "pending review should be curtained off to public til
walkthrough." In VR, a room or element under review is curtained off —
not shown to the public — until it has had its walkthrough (review
pass). The veil in the pilot's honesty grammar is a true curtain:
contents behind it are not displayed. (The flat site's pending-banner
practice is a separate, earlier ruling and is not changed by this.)

## DG-W1 — WebGPU tier sequencing: DECIDED BY ATLAS (delegated)

Carter delegated this one ("you decide"). Atlas's decision: the WebGPU
tier is built AFTER the all-rooms waves (Phase 2B stays after Phase 2).
Reason: completeness before richness — the pilot and waves certify on
the WebGL2 build the headsets actually run; a graphics tier built
before all rooms exist would need re-proving wave by wave; the
Every-Screen rule already guarantees weaker devices the same content
in lighter form. Reversible by Carter at any time.
