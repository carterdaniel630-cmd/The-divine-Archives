Systemic, pass-wide: (a) one house style poured over every culture —
no Egyptian canon, no cuneiform/glazed brick, no Greek sculptural
grammar, no Maya/Khmer/Chinese grammar (Mogao tower reads as a red
office block; Angkor naga = plain rail). The pass's per-site work
should be specified in each tradition's OWN artistic language.
(b) No time or wear anywhere: uniform clean stone, no water/wind
logic, no foot-wear — the pass should carry an ageing logic per site.
(c) Felt scale: empty flat foregrounds miniaturise monuments
(Persepolis, Borobudur, Chichen, Alexandria) — foreground dressing
and human-scale reference belong in the pass's judgement.
(d) The Rotunda planetarium dome is a FLAT star-chart: constellation
lines run straight with no dome distortion, Pegasus is outlined twice
and the two outlines disagree, labels stay upright while the sky
rotates, FIGURES toggle draws no figures. Dome geometry joins the
Rotunda's fix-batch lighting item as the room's artistic half.
