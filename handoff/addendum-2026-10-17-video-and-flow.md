## 2. Production split — 3 animations + 3 stills-with-motion per day

**Daily production is 6 videos total:**
- **3 full animations** — full generated motion, characters where needed, everything in motion, not stills.
- **3 stills-with-motion** — multi-scene motion: several stills, crossfades / parallax / kinetic captions, never one still plus slow zoom.

**Rules for both kinds:**
- House voice throughout (TruthTeller, deep, warm, human). One idea, one payoff, 30–45 seconds. Hook in the first second (picture + on-screen text + spoken line). Loop devices (open loop, retention reset near 25–30 seconds, split-sentence loop, audio cut at the last syllable). Kinetic captions, burned-in captions, sound-off first. Music low under the story, silent on the payoff and the end tag.
- Honest hooks only: withhold the answer, never the context; label myth / legend / debate. No invented quotes. No presenting legend as fact.
- Original visuals only. Characters are original — designed from the Archives' own descriptions, never copying films, shows, or other people's characters.
- No repeats. Fresh topics only, working through the chapters.

**What this supersedes:** any v2 line that reads as "daily pipeline keeps running the settled format" or "tonight's 5 post as-is." Those lines were true when v2 was written. They are now superseded by the hold + re-tag + approval flow below.

## 3. Storyline videos — a new kind

**Storyline videos are short-form story videos:**
- One story per video, told as a story, narrated start to finish, based on what is in the Archives.
- Full animation characters (original only), motion everywhere — not stills.
- Same shape as other videos: 30–45 seconds, hook → three beats → payoff → final end tag with Follow and Share. Same voice, captions, sound-off-first, and honesty rules as §2.
- Truth: told as "the tradition tells..." / "in the story...", with myth, legend, and history labeled. Never present legend as fact. Never invent quotes.
- Sacred care: some traditions do not want their sacred figures shown. For those stories, use silhouettes, symbols, and landscapes instead of faces — or pick a different story. Flag it per story.

**First storyline demo — PICKED: Anansi.**
Anansi the trickster spider (West African / Akan tradition) — the story of Anansi earning the world's stories from Nyame, the sky god. Told as the tradition tells it. Building now. It goes to pending for Carter's approval. It ships only after Carter clicks approve.

**Where storyline videos sit in the split:** storyline videos are the natural home of the 3 animation slots, but the animation slots may also hold non-storyline animations (for example, the next batch's Ragnarok, Anubis, and Dreamtime / Rainbow Serpent). Do not force every animation to be a storyline video.
