LIUBO · 六博

THE GAME OF SIX STICKS — HAN CHINA, c. 2ND CENTURY BCE

⚠ THE BOARD IS REAL. THE RULES ARE A RECONSTRUCTION.

The TLV-patterned board below is attested: boards carved with the T, L and V marks survive from Han tombs (notably a lacquered board from Mawangdui) and stone engravings. No complete set of Liubo's rules survives. Every rule, throw table and win condition in this exhibit is a modern scholarly reconstruction — of the family associated with Yang Hongxun and Donald Harper's research — and is labeled as such throughout. Nothing presented here as a rule should be mistaken for historical fact.

RULES OF PLAY

Your move — jade Jade 0 · Ivory 0

Each side holds six chessmen. They race around the twelve marked points of the circuit and off the far end. You (jade) enter at the green-gated point and travel sunwise; the archive's player (ivory) enters opposite and travels the same circuit against you. RECONSTRUCTED

CAPTURE & WATER RECONSTRUCTED

  • Landing on an enemy piece captures it — it returns to its owner's hand. A capture earns a bonus throw.
  • The two deep-blue stations are water: pieces there are safe from capture.
  • A piece must land exactly to leave the board; an overshooting throw cannot use that piece.
  • Victory: first to bring all six pieces around the full circuit and off the board wins.

THE TLV BOARD ATTESTED

The T, L and V inlay marks are drawn from the archaeological record — the Mawangdui lacquer board and Han stone engravings. The two tall standing pieces beside the center mark echo the oversized "chessmen" found in Han tombs; their exact function in the game is unknown.

CHRONICLE

Liubo (六博, "six sticks") was the most popular board game of the Han dynasty, played by emperors and commoners alike, and later a symbol of the soul's journey. Its rules were already fragmentary by the Tang dynasty. This exhibit follows the reconstruction family associated with Yang Hongxun and Donald Harper, simplified for play: one circuit, six sticks, fixed throw values. The board's TLV geometry is attested; everything else is honest reconstruction. Crafted for The Divine Archives.
