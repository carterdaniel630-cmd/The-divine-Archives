The Two Sides

One hunts. The other endures. Neither can win the other's way.

Tigers — 4. Step along any line, or leap over an adjacent goat to the empty point beyond, taking it. Win by capturing 5 goats.

Goats — 20. Placed one per turn, then stepped along the lines. They never jump and never capture. Win by leaving every tiger with no legal move.

The Flow of Play

Goats move first, placing one goat each turn while the tigers prowl. Once all twenty stand on the board, the goats too may step — and the long siege begins.

Rules of the game — what is attested, what is reconstructed

Attested rules

  • AttestedThe board is a 5×5 grid of 25 points, every square crossed by diagonals; pieces stand on the intersections and move along the drawn lines.
  • AttestedFour tigers begin on the four corner points. All twenty goats begin off the board.
  • AttestedThe goat player moves first, placing one goat per turn on any empty point. The tigers move on their own turns throughout this placement phase.
  • AttestedA tiger steps to an adjacent empty point along a line, or captures by jumping over an adjacent goat to the empty point immediately beyond — as in draughts. Capturing is not obligatory.
  • AttestedGoats never jump and never capture. Once all twenty are placed, a goat steps to an adjacent empty point along a line.
  • AttestedTigers win by capturing five goats — the common threshold; some circles play to four.
  • AttestedGoats win by blocking all four tigers' legal moves.

Reconstructed in this exhibit

  • ReconstructedA capture may chain several jumps within one turn while further jumps are available. Some versions permit only one capture per turn; the chained form is the common house rule used here.
  • ReconstructedDraws by threefold repetition, or after 100 quiet half-moves, are a modern safeguard against endless play — not a traditional rule.
  • ReconstructedA few folk boards omit some of the diagonals; this exhibit uses the standard full-diagonal board.
  • ReconstructedThe computer opponent's heuristics are modern inventions, tuned for a lively game rather than perfect play.
