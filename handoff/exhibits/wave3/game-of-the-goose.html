Chronicle of the game

    The rules, honestly told

    The course Attested
    Two travellers race along a spiral of 63 squares, casting two dice each turn. The first to land on square 63 by an exact throw wins. If your throw would carry you past 63, you go in to 63 and bounce back the excess.
    The geese Attested
    Squares 5, 9, 14, 18, 23, 27, 32, 36, 41, 45, 50, 54, 59 bear the goose — the classic scheme of the standard boards, and 63 itself is shown as a goose in the garden. Land on one and you advance again by the same throw; a second goose carries you yet again. Should you meet a goose while bouncing back from 63, it drives you further back by the excess instead.
    The hazards Attested
    6 · The Bridge — cross at once to 12. 19 · The Inn — tarry and rest. 31 · The Well — climb out slowly. 42 · The Maze — lose the path, return to 30. 52 · The Prison — wait behind bars. 58 · Death — begin the whole journey over.
    The rests Reconstructed
    Early editions disagree on the penalties — some keep you in the Well or Prison until another player arrives. For a two-player table we use fixed rests: the Inn costs 1 turn, the Well 2 turns, the Prison 3 turns. The Maze returns you to 30 (some editions say 39).
    The first cast Attested
    On the very first cast of the game, a throw of nine is a famous opening: 6 + 3 flies to 26, 5 + 4 flies to 53. A roll-off of single dice decides who casts first.
    What we simplified Reconstructed
    No stakes or pot; two players only (the old game welcomed more); landing on an occupied square does not swap the travellers, as some editions have it. Nothing here is presented as fact beyond what the badges claim.
