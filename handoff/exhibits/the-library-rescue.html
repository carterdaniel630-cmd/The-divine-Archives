What the record allows us to say

The Mouseion

The Library belonged to Alexandria’s Mouseion: a royal institution of scholarship, dining, teaching, and collection. Strabo’s Geography describes the Mouseion as part of the palace quarter, with a covered walk, an arcade, and a common hall for its learned members.

Not one cinematic fire

The Library’s losses unfolded across centuries. Caesar’s campaign in 48 BCE involved a fire near the harbor; ancient accounts disagree on its reach. Fighting under Aurelian in the third century damaged the Brucheion district. Later religious and political upheavals changed the city again. No surviving source gives us the single all-consuming blaze of legend.
