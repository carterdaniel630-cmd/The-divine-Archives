The Free Kitchen — a living practice of the Sikh faith
  1. Cover your head and slip off your shoes — as every visitor does.
  2. Wash your hands at the basin by the door.
  3. Take a steel plate and join the line. No tickets. No payment. No questions.
  4. Sit on the floor in the row — beside strangers, as equals.
This is a living practice, happening today in gurdwaras around the world.
Walk it with respect.
