THE MITHRAEUM

Descend into a cave-temple of Mithras, god of the unconquered sun
A note on honesty. No Mithraic scripture survives — no hymns, no doctrine, nothing the cult itself wrote about its beliefs. What we know comes from archaeology: underground halls like the one beneath San Clemente in Rome, the tauroctony reliefs, and a list of seven grades recorded by St. Jerome. Everything about the rites is reconstructed and is marked as such here. This walkthrough is observational — you are a witness, not an initiate.
