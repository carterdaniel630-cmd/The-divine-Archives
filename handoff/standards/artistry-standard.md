# Artistry Standard — The Divine Archives (absolute highest creative and scholarly quality bar)

Ratified by the user October 12, 2026 (refined same day). Companion to the
Modular Framework Standard. We do not ship flat, generic 2D layouts or
sterile UI components. Every visual asset, 3D mesh, texture, and animation
routine is treated with museum-grade artistry under these parameters —
while holding the performance bounds (vanilla modular ES6 JS, explicit
.dispose() destruction lifecycles, forced 1.0 devicePixelRatio cap on
mobile viewports). Output pure code with no conversational filler.

## 1. Visual depth & dimension
Every element must possess true 3D spatial depth. If building in Three.js,
utilize advanced material properties (roughness, metalness, and subtle
normal mapping) to give surfaces a tactile, weathered, or polished weight.
Avoid basic flat shading entirely.

## 2. Historical rigor & iconography
All symbolic markings, glyphs, and decorative borders must be rendered via
custom, precise SVG paths or high-resolution procedural textures based
strictly on primary texts and archaeological records (e.g., true zodiac
glyph signatures or exact manuscript seals). Never substitute geometric
approximations or fantasy shapes for sacred or historical symbols.

## 3. Motion & séance pacing
Structural transitions, lighting reveals, and user interactions must utilize
fluid, mathematically elegant easing curves. Implement intentional
micro-interactions — delicate atmospheric particle drifts, soft emissive
runic glows pulsing from dark stone, and deliberate pacing that respects
the solemn, stoic voice of the archive.

## 4. Objective lighting (chiaroscuro)
The lighting schemas must open with high-contrast cinematic drama without
losing structural clarity. Balance rich, dark tones with targeted spot
illumination that pulls the viewer's eye exactly to the focal interactive
objects (e.g., the lens of a planchette or the circle bounds of a ritual
floor).

## 5. The artisan's compliance
Deliver this absolute standard of high-fidelity craftsmanship while
adhering perfectly to the performance bounds. Output pure code with no
conversational filler.

## Standing audit note
Clause 2 is retroactive: the Ouija Board's geometric zodiac medallions are
a known violation — true individually-legible glyph signatures required,
drawn from the historical record, not invented.
The Craft Council audits all artifacts against all five parameters.
