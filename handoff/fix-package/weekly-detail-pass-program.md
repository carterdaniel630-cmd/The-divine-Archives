# Weekly Detail-Pass Program — standing rule (Carter 2026-10-16)

Games get a detail pass ONCE A WEEK. Every week, on schedule, the games
are reviewed honestly, compared against similar games, and made better.
This is a standing program, not a one-off.

## What the pass covers

1. **Mechanics, honestly.** Play each game and name what is stiff,
   touchy, unclear, boring, or impossible — the way Carter names it
   (Tomb Robber's spikes, Divine Casualties' stiff feel). No softening.
   If a jump cannot be made, a button cannot be read, or a character
   cannot be seen, that goes on the fix list in plain words.
2. **Detail.** Every game gets more detail: richer visuals, clearer
   feedback, better sound, more readable everything. Detail is not
   decoration — it is the player always knowing what is happening and
   why.
3. **Comparison.** Every week, at least one game gets a full comparison
   against similar games (see below). Over time, every game gets
   compared.
4. **Truth labels.** Reconstructions stay labeled as reconstructions.
   Attested vs. reconstructed vs. modern convention — the labels never
   come off to make a game feel more "real."

## How comparison works

1. Pick the game, then pick 2–3 similar games (same genre or same
   mechanics — other board games, other racers, other platformers).
2. Play them. List what they do BETTER than ours: controls, feel,
   readability, detail, fun.
3. List what ours does BETTER than them: keep those strengths.
4. Close the gap AND exceed: fix what they do better, then add one
   thing they do not have — detail, feel, or a mechanic that fits our
   tradition.
5. Write it down: per-game fix list + detail additions, in plain
   words, filed for Claude.

## What the pass delivers

- A written fix list per game + detail additions, filed each week.
- At least one full comparison (with named similar games) per week.
- A phone-first check: every fix verified on a phone screen, because
  that is where Carter plays and reviews.

## Rules

- Do not strip engaging capabilities for pure accuracy (creative
  renditions stay engaging — label departures instead).
- Board-game form rule stands: board games look and play like board
  games, mystical feel matching their tradition.
- Nothing ships from the Muse side as final. The pass produces fix
  lists; Claude Code rebuilds.
