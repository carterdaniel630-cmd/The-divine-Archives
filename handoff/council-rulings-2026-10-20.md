# Council Rulings Record — The Museum Constitution
**Session:** 2026-10-20 (27-agent council, convened at Carter's order; advisory brief: `2026-10-20-advisory-brief-museum-constitution.md`)
**Ruled by:** Carter, decision by decision, 2026-10-20 evening.
**Status:** These rulings are binding build law. Several decisions were reshaped or replaced by Carter's own designs — where his design differs from the council's options, HIS design is the ruling.

## The decisions

**1. The Tjurunga — TAKE IT DOWN + SWEEP THE VAULT.** Ruled within the hour; verified in five live places; the sweep of all 88 Vault items found it the only certain takedown. Removal order staged separately (`handoff/tjurunga-takedown-order-2026-10-20.md`). Chapter prose discussing the restriction stays (discussion of a boundary is not display across it) unless Carter later rules otherwise.

**2. Where games live — CARTER'S DESIGN (replaces both council options).** The game LIVES in its room (found and anchored there, made from the room's own material). Click to play takes the visitor to the ARCADE, where the game is played (one build per game). On exit, the visitor stands in the arcade, free to play other games. Two doors out: back to the room they came from, or out into the museum via the BRONZE AGE hallway (the hidden arcade's golden-archway entrance). Doors are labelled with their destinations; arriving from a room lands the visitor in front of that game.

**3. Room eggs — DEFERRED.** No room eggs are built now. They come later, after the build-out, once the rooms' information is in place (an egg is made from the room's information, so the information comes first). The one-programme-or-two question is moot until then. Chapter eggs are a separate existing programme — untouched.

**4. Witness moments — CARTER'S DESIGN.** A WITNESS MOMENT FOR EVERY ROOM: a hidden encounter space reached by a portal in the room; the tradition's rite/moment unfolds in a space set for it; the visitor is present and the space is PARTIALLY INTERACTIVE at the edges (tend, carry, light, answer — the Fire Temple pattern); then back to the room. The full room set is universal; the FORM bends by kind; for restricted rooms the witness moment may be a threshold/absence moment. **Clarified the same evening: a witness moment is NOT a game and never counts as or substitutes for the room's game — the two are separate obligations of every room.**

**5. The Rotunda — CARTER'S DESIGN (replaces all four council wordings).** Nothing lives in the Rotunda. It is a ROTATING FEATURED SHOWCASE: displays drawn from the home rooms, swapped on a rotation (roughly weekly or every two weeks; exact cadence set at build time), sometimes spanning several rooms with sources cited, always pointing back into the home room(s). The 7 permanent Rotunda theme rooms dissolve into the feature system. The Rotunda stays the entrance: Grand Directory + what's featured now.

**6. Syncretism Clause — ADOPTED.** Where a room's tradition is itself a mixture (African Diaspora Religions; Mandaeans/Yazidis/Druze; Akbar & the Mughal Synthesis), mixture counts as the room's own information; its game and witness moment may span traditions as the room's settled scholarship says the tradition itself does.

**7a. Music — STAYS PARKED.** A room's air is built from the natural sounds of the place, its echo and materials, documented non-musical sounds (bells, water, tools, footsteps), and deliberate silence where silence is the truth.

**7b. Video sound test — TRIED, THEN CLOSED.** One test video (Teotihuacan, desert-wind bed) was made at Carter's order; on hearing it he ruled: NO background sounds in videos — voice over ONLY. Closed for good.

**8. Pilot room — EARLY CHINA / ORACLE BONES OF ANYANG.** One room proven end to end under the constitution, honestly costed, Carter's eye as judge, before anything scales. (The Oracle Bones relic is already built and in the armory.)

**9. Finish line + room kit — ADOPTED.** Every room climbs: Listed → Sourced → Stocked → Playable → Conforming. Each room carries a one-page kit approved by Carter once per room: identity card, stocking list, air dossier (light / colour / sound / materials / fullness), game seed, witness-moment seed, lived-practice note, honest-gaps page. Videos: a one-page information sheet before any script (see 15.1 for the corrected sourcing chain).

**10. Approvals — PER ITEM through the pilot.** No past approval carries over to a game's new status as a room's game. Batching never applies to living/restricted rooms.

**11. Living practices — SYSTEM ADOPTED, STRICT DEFAULT.** Every practice sorted Perform (the faith itself opens it to anyone) / Witness (present, not enacting) / Learn (objects and knowledge around the rite). Hard lines: no visitor performs baptism, communion, conversion testimony, initiation, marriage, or funeral rites; no scores, streaks, timers, or completion percentages on any practice, ever; a written Withheld List per living/restricted room, checked before stocking. Mara + Iris's lines adopted: never play the persecutor in the Witch Trials room; living rites are never collectible; undeciphered stays undeciphered. **The default when the welcome is unclear: WATCH ONLY (Tess's line) unless the invitation is documented.**

**12. Figures — RESHAPED BY CARTER'S ROTUNDA CIRCLE + ADOPTED PARTS.**
- **The Rotunda Circle (Carter's design):** TWELVE statues stand around the Rotunda's walls — SIX philosophers and SIX deities, ALTERNATING deity–philosopher–deity–philosopher, encircling the room. The six + six are not yet picked; statue design comes later.
- In the rooms: each figure gets a PLAQUE + EMBLEM, in the room's own order, until character work begins.
- The per-room standing-figure cap dissolved (statues live in the Rotunda, not in rooms).
- TEXT-ONLY RULE adopted now: no depiction of Muhammad (name in calligraphy, biography instead); the God of Israel is never a built character (the Name as text). Applied room by room when the character phase comes, never as a blanket build.

**13. Philosophers — CARTER'S RULING (closes the council's question).** Philosophers are NEVER placed in the religious rooms — no philosopher plaques in tradition rooms, no relation labels, no plaque remap. Philosophers have THEIR OWN AREA of the museum, separate from everything else. The only place philosophers mix with religions and beliefs is the ROTUNDA (the statue ring) — and nothing lives in the Rotunda; it is showcase, not residence. (Supersedes the older "plaques in relevant rooms, no dedicated room" plan.)

**14a. The debate duel's ending — A CLEAN WINNER (Leo + Milo's side).** One flame stands; the other's goes out. The respect rule still holds: effects land on arguments, never on faiths or peoples.

**14b. The 900 Theses — HOLD LIFTED.** Asked whether the Oct 17 hold (a simple park, never a blocker) covered Pico's debate-table station, Carter lifted the park entirely: the programme — the room AND Station 16 together — is back on the active work list. First work: the prepared spec-body patch aligning the spec to his rulings, Gate A text extraction, translation licensing review.

**15. Video rules — RULED IN FOUR PARTS.**
1. **Information first (Carter's clarification):** the documented record is the root. Rooms are built from it; videos are built from the same information in turn. Room and video are siblings, both answerable to the record. A script's source sheet is an information sheet, not a room-inventory sheet.
2. **Look: decided per design.** Each video's visual look is decided when that video is designed, case by case. No blanket per-tradition dialect system imposed up front.
3. **Closing tag-box (Carter's design):** on the closing frame only, a SMALL text tag in a box at the bottom of the screen — very briefly, where the subject sits inside the museum (room/wing) and where to find the museum (getconexto.com). Text only, never spoken. The spoken end line stays exactly and only "The past is still speaking."
4. **Never from an unsourced topic (hard rule):** no video (or game) from a topic with nothing documented behind it, or whose writing is still pending review. Source before building, every time.
- Grounding fact stated with this decision: **the videos live on social media only** — not in the museum, not on the website. The "approved videos stand until rooms are stocked" timing idea is dead.

**16. Directory honesty — BOTH ADOPTED.**
(a) **Public scorecard:** per-room figure count, relic count, and honest game state (Play it / In the Arcade hall / Not yet — being made from this room). No egg field, ever. (The pairs' timing split — publish now vs after the first rooms stock — was not separately ruled; it follows the build order: paper in Phase 0, live with Claude's builds.)
(b) **"Not Yet Housed" register:** a visible register naming the major uncovered traditions (32 by the topic map) — a named absence is honest; a silent absence reads as a verdict. Standalone YORUBA room first (a living tradition of tens of millions, currently folded inside a generic label — itself a violation of "nothing generic"), Bön and Cao Dai named as absences until full chapters stock them, Stoicism via the Philosophers/Debate Hall track with museum cross-reference. Standing rule: **no new room without a full chapter behind it** — chapter → withheld list → room shell → stocking, never shell-first.
