## Fact spine (the land carries Ellora's own record only)
- Carved from a single basalt cliff, **top-down** — excavated downward, so every pillar, stair, chamber and sculpture emerged from the stone itself; nothing was assembled.
- Commissioned under the Rashtrakuta king Krishna I, 8th century CE (c. 756–773). Dedicated to Shiva; its court and tower evoke Mount Kailash.
- The commonly cited estimate of ~200,000 tons of rock removed is carried **as an estimate**, labelled as one.
- Ellora as a whole: 34 caves — Buddhist, Hindu, Jain — one cliff, three faiths side by side; UNESCO World Heritage (inscribed 1983).
- **Kept separate, by fact:** the musical pillars in the Reel that prompted this commission belong to the Vittala Temple at Hampi, NOT Ellora. This land does not borrow another temple's marvels.
- The Reel's "forgotten technology / sound and vibration" framing is its speculation, not site copy. House register: wonder at the attested craft; open questions stay labelled as questions.
