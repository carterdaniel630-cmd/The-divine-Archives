# The Perfection Standard — adopted as standing law (Carter, 2026-10-26)

Carter adopted this 2026-10-26 (~10:53 ET) in chat, in his words: one person
viewing and verifying a massive project means "there should be a level of
perfection that we adhere to."

Full text: maintained on the Muse side at build-standards/perfection-standard.md.
Substance for the loop:

- Every piece climbs the ladder before it is presented to Carter: (1) built
  from its own documented source, nothing generic; (2) maker's feature-by-
  feature check, defects reported; (3) INDEPENDENT check — the maker never
  grades their own work alone (the loop already runs reviewer/auditor; this
  makes the principle explicit law, alongside the auditor-proof gate Carter
  ruled on 2026-10-26); (4) atlas's eye at Carter's zoom before presentation;
  (5) Carter's verdict, which is the last rung, not the only one.
- Drift control: the same defect on two pieces becomes a named pattern,
  written into standing checks and swept for on waiting pieces.
- Claims and counts come from the thing itself (the room's own data, the
  Page, the live site entered as a visitor enters it), never from a report
  about the thing.
- "Done" means: passed every rung AND Carter said yes. Anything less is
  "built," "checked," or "waiting."
- Applies to every line: relics, figures, videos, pages, games, staging
  files, and any adopted outside proposal. No exceptions for speed.
