# THE KEEPER — THE CHECKLIST

Every picture, model, or build of the Keeper is judged against this list, line by line. Carter's eye is the final check. Assembled 2026-10-29 from the design record and Carter's rulings.

## Face and head
- [ ] Face is a pure black void — no eyes, no nose, no mouth, ever
- [ ] Deep hood
- [ ] Halo disc behind his head: SOLID, with physical weight (not linework alone), engraved with moon phases and sacred geometry

## Robe and body
- [ ] Heavy, floor-length dark robe with real material weight and folds
- [ ] Embroidery: sacred-geometry linework and ancient symbols of many traditions, worked in aged bronze-and-gold thread — weathered, oxidized, worn thin in places, some symbols partially faded
- [ ] NO flowers or floral patterns on the robe
- [ ] Moon and stars stay
- [ ] Bare feet
- [ ] Palette: deep browns, charcoal, dark bronze, muted gold — no bright colors, no reds, no greens

## Hands and pose
- [ ] RIGHT hand raised in the teaching gesture — thumb tip touching index tip — in every view (one frozen moment, seen from three sides)
- [ ] Left hand resting open at his side
- [ ] Nothing carried in his hands — no pole, no staff (the ferryman gear is struck)

## Companion
- [ ] The raven is with him — near-black plumage, muted bronze sheen, large enough to read as a bird at phone size
- [ ] The raven is wordless; the Keeper is the voice

## Nature
- [ ] The death-and-renewal wake: the ground dies where he steps — withered grass, browned leaves, dulled stone — and rejuvenates a half-step behind him, green returning, small flowers opening
- [ ] The edge between death and renewal feathers like a tide mark — never a hard split line
- [ ] He emanates solemn calm, never menace. He is kind. Death in him is part of the cycle, not a threat.

## Who he is (the rules no picture may break)
- [ ] He never gates. The museum is a free space — he offers introductions, crossings, and answers; he never requires accompaniment. ("No one crosses unaccompanied" is struck, permanently.)
- [ ] He teaches only from the records — every chapter, relic, and record the Archive holds. Where the record is silent, he says so; contested things are taught as contested.
- [ ] His purpose, in Carter's words: "it's all for him to help us make the divine archives better as a whole. what he learns is what helps."
