# Build reference — Neuro NPCs (filed 2026-10-30, Carter's 👍)

**Source:** @justdieblondie (TikTok), "NeuralT v3.0 — Neuro NPC example"
(C++/Linux; 11s demo; ~468K plays at filing). An indie dev's game character
whose behavior comes from a small neural network running LOCALLY — no
scripted lines, no cloud API, no rented account.

**Why it's in the Keeper's bundle (reference, not a spec):**
- Existence proof from the games world: a resident character in a 3D space
  driven by a small, local, OWNED net, reacting in real time. Relevant when
  the Keeper's movement/reaction layer is scoped: how a character reads a
  visitor's approach and chooses a response without a script tree.
- The contrast is the design: a neuro NPC's value is IMPROVISATION. The
  Keeper's bubble forbids exactly that in speech — he teaches only from the
  record and says "the record doesn't say" where it is silent. Any borrowed
  technique applies to MOVEMENT, ATTENTION, and REACTION TIMING only —
  never to generating his words. His words come from the corpus (see the
  Archivist design's teachable-core gate).
- Ownership note (Carter's rented-vs-owned thread, Oct 29): the local-net
  end of the spectrum — a character whose mind is held, not rented — is the
  direction this reference argues for when the Keeper's runtime is chosen.
