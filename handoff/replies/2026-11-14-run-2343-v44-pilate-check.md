## What the sources say (opened this run, 2026-11-14)
1. **CIIP II 1277**, in the *Judaism and Rome* database
   (https://heurist.huma-num.fr/heurist/judaism_and_rome/view/288 and
   https://www.judaism-and-rome.org/dedication-pontius-pilate-judea-ciip-ii-1277): **height 82 cm, width 65 cm,
   depth 20 cm**, letters 6 cm; Israel Museum, inv. IAA 1961-529. Diplomatic text:
   `[---]S TIBERIEVM / [----]NTIVS PILATVS / [-----]ECTVS IVDA[--]E / [-----]E[-------]`.
   Line 4: only one letter is visible, "an apex above a vertical stroke", read as E. Alföldy restores `[ref]eci[t]`
   ("restored"); Taylor (NTS 52, 2006) suggests the E begins *dedicavit*.
2. **Wikipedia, "Pilate stone"** (secondary, no reference on the figures): infobox "Height: 82 cm, Width: 65 cm";
   line 4 given as `[...FECIT D]E[DICAVIT]` (citing Chilton and Evans 1998, p. 465), i.e. also only the E outside brackets.

No source opened gives the block as 82 wide × 65 high. The Israel Museum's own record could not be found by search
from this session; not opened, said so.

## Findings
**F1. Model orientation (museum, `docs/museum/relics.js` `CAT.v44`).** The model is `w = 0.82, h = 0.65` (landscape);
the stone is 82 high × 65 wide (portrait). Depth 0.2 is right. The inscription texture (PR #125) is placed for a
landscape face, so a fix is a swap of w and h plus re-fitting the four lines (font size from H, right edge from W)
and a headless check at 390 and 1280. Order `pilate-stone-texture-fix-order.md` forbade geometry changes in #125, so
this needs Carter's word.

**F2. Vault V44 line 4 (live page `docs/vault/pilate-stone.html`, lines 82 and 92, generated by `tools/build-vault.js`
from the vault data).** The page prints `[ . . . FE]CIT D[E . . . ]` and `[…FE]CIT D[E…]`, which marks "CIT D" as
surviving. Both sources above show only one surviving letter, E. The page's own note ("letters in brackets are
restorations") makes this an over-statement of what the stone shows. Suggested honest wording (exact, for a later build):
- inscription line 4: `[ . . . ]E[ . . . ]` (static: `[…]E[…]`);
- one added sentence under the text: "Only one letter of the last line survives, an E. Géza Alföldy restored it as
  *[ref]eci[t]*, 'restored'; others read the start of *dedicavit*, 'dedicated'."
- gloss "made/dedicated" → "restored/dedicated"; "built or dedicated a Tiberieum" → "built, restored or dedicated a
  Tiberieum".
- source log: add the CIIP / *Judaism and Rome* record to the "The stone" sources (it is already in the Museum
  rendition section on `main`).
The page has no pending tag (V44 is cleared), so the change re-opens a cleared entry, like the V36 link question.

**Smaller, no action proposed:** the "82 × 65 cm" on the page is right as written (no orientation stated). The page's
"What's genuinely open" already lists the wording of the last line.
