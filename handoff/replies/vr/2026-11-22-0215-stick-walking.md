  the side, 2 m away, at text sizes 1.47° and 1.96°.
- **Turning it on:**
  - one press asks, and the plaque warns **MORE INTENSE** ("The room glides past you; some people feel queasy");
  - confirmation 1 of 2, then 2 of 2, each needing at least 0.5 s since the last press, so a double press can't rush through;

  - off is one press;
  - the choice is kept on this device (`mu-vr-walk`).

  - it speeds up from 0 to 2.0 m/s over 1 s and stops at 4 m/s² (0.45 m after letting go);
  - movement follows the head's facing, level, with no roll and no height change;
  - a wall stops the visitor or they slide along it, never through;
  - there is a dead zone for a resting thumb;
  - the vignette stays on for the whole glide (Comfort §b.7 "any artificial motion"; the interaction spec's 1.2 m/s line falls inside that).

- **Only a right controller:** the plaque says "needs the left controller", and that stick keeps turning.
