# VR loop run 2026-11-17 ~05:47–06:30 UTC — light rig per tier (PR #182)

**Built:** PR #182, `loop/vr-light-rig` `118abdcc`, on `main` `5570e888`. VR-STATUS Next 1.
Spec: `vr-performance-budget.md` §2 as settled in AM-PER-01.

**What it does.** These changes apply in a headset session only:
- the lights drop to 5 on the floor tier (Quest 2 and unknown headsets) and to 6 on Quest 3;
- the rig is 1 hemisphere + ambient + the visitor's lantern + 2 pooled lamp lights (3 on Quest 3);
- the lightning hemisphere is switched off, and its flash is added into the main hemisphere (three.js sums
  colour × intensity, so surfaces light the same);
- lights are switched off, not set to 0, because three.js still evaluates a light at 0 in every pixel;
- the rig changes only at session start and end, so shaders rebuild once at entry, not during a flash.

The flat museum keeps its 8 lights, and they are restored on leaving VR. `museum.html` cache key moves to `v=56`, ahead
of the waiting stack (52–55). A debug hook, `__MU.lights()`, reports the rig.
