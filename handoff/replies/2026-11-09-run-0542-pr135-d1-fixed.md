## What changed (D1)
1. **A middle level of detail.** Each statue now has three levels, picked by its own distance to the visitor's eye:
   near mesh within 3.2 m; **middle copy 3.2–8 m** (Shiva 25,702 / Isis 24,146 / Socrates 26,026 triangles); far copy
   beyond 8 m (8,889 / 7,846 / 7,381). The ~6k copy no longer shows where a visitor walks between statues.
2. **Better copies.** Still vertex clustering on one grid per statue, but each merged point is placed where it best
   keeps the surfaces around it (area-weighted plane fit, Lindstrom's out-of-core simplification; falls back to the
   average when ill-defined or outside its cell), so faces, crown, trident and drum keep their edges.
3. **Pinholes.** Copies are shaded with the averaged source normals (computed normals only where they cancel), and
   any triangle whose winding came out reversed is turned back. No pinholes visible in the 360/390 shots.
4. **Room for the middle copy in the budget.** Near copies are capped at 95k (Shiva 106,936 → 94,904; Socrates
   132,060 → 94,553; Isis 75,000 unchanged). Side by side at 1.4 m and 2 m, the 95k Socrates is not visibly different
   from the full mesh (`r1f2-socrates-near-full-vs-95k-390.jpg`: full, 95k, full, 95k). The jagged robe edges and
   bands on Socrates are in the full mesh too (also visible in `fix-near-socrates-390.png`): they come from the
   made-for-the-statue robe, not from the copies. Statue pages still turn the full meshes.
5. **Shorter load stall.** Each copy is made in its own task, and the triangle de-duplication uses numeric keys.
   With the CPU slowed 4× (headless, 2 runs each): longest main-thread task **1.2–1.3 s** (was 1.9–2.0 s on `9d82ed28`);
   all three ready 8–9 s after arriving at the Rotunda door (was 5–6 s; statues are fetched from 22 m out, so the visitor
   is still walking in). Not measured on a real phone.
