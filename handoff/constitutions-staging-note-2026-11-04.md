## How the set binds the loop
1. The constitutions are STANDING LAW for the loop, read where he stands,
   mid-build — not reference material to consult after.
2. THE ORDER OF AUTHORITY (from the INDEX): Carter's live word overrides
   everything written, including the INDEX; then the Bubbled Law (00);
   then the system's own constitution; habit governs nothing — where
   habit and a constitution differ, the constitution wins and the habit
   is corrected.
3. WHERE A CONSTITUTION AND A BUILD ORDER CONFLICT, THE CONSTITUTION
   WINS. The build stops on that point; the conflict is flagged back
   through the handoff channel for atlas and Carter. The loop does not
   improvise around a conflict and does not silently resolve one.
4. THE FIVE KNOWN PAPER CONTRADICTIONS in the house record (named in the
   2026-11-01 drafting report — among them the contract's merge text vs
   merge-on-green, and the council's 27-vs-28 seats) are CARTER'S to
   resolve. They are not the loop's to fix, harmonise, or build around:
   flag, don't fix. Where one of them touches a build, the constitution
   text as staged is the operative text until Carter rules otherwise.
5. PROVENANCE stands as written: articles marked GATHERED restate
   Carter's existing rulings; articles flagged DRAFTED are in force with
   the set, their flags kept as provenance. The loop does not re-flag,
   re-draft, or tidy them.
