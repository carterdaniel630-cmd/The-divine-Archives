1. **The website is the well.** Every chapter, Vault entry, figure, symbol, game, and room built into getconexto.com becomes source material that can be drawn from again and again.
2. **Everything built feeds everything else.** A chapter becomes shorts, a podcast episode, a book section, a game, a product. One piece of research, many forms — each form points back to the well.
3. **Volume compounds.** More in the well → more to draw from → more content in every market → more reach → more reason and more material to build back into the site. The two-year horizon is this wheel turning daily.

- **The Standard holds at scale.** Massive output means massive output *at the floor* — Shiva-level for design, the Vestal Virgins bar for video. Volume never dilutes the bar; a piece below it does not ship, however many are moving.
- **The verdict gate stands.** Nothing public without Carter's yes on that piece. Scale comes from the system (night builds, day waves, the whole machine used at capacity), never from skipping his eye.
