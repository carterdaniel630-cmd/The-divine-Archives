Method, conventions & honesty

Gematria is an interpretive tradition — a way of listening for resonance between words — not a method of prediction. What this instrument reckons, it reckons openly:

  • Hebrew follows Mispar Hechrechi (Aleph = 1 through Tav = 400); final (sofit) forms take their standard values. Some schools instead value finals 500–900.
  • Greek follows the Milesian system (Alpha = 1 through Sampi = 900), including digamma, koppa, and sampi.
  • Arabic follows the standard abjad order (Alif = 1 through Ghayn = 1000).
  • Judgment calls, flagged not hidden: ة (tāʾ marbūṭa) is counted 400 — some schools count 5; ى (alif maqṣūra) is counted 10 — some count 1; standalone hamza ء is set aside; Latin “ch” renders as ח (Chet = 8), though in words like baruch the כ (Kaf = 20) some would expect is a different letter.
  • The Arabic ring carries the 28 base letters; orthographic variants remain available in the typed fallback.

This instrument is a modern devotional imagining in the lineage of volvelles and counting boards. It claims no ancient provenance: no historical gematria calculating machine is known to have existed.
