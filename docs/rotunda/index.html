Statues for the museum’s Rotunda

The Rotunda Circle

Recently added · pending review

Recently added · pending full review. These statue pages are live but have not yet completed the keeper’s review pass. Wording and detail may still change. The tag is removed once each page is cleared.

A ring of statues is being made for the Rotunda of the museum, a few at a time. Each one can be turned in 3D on its own page, and each carries a plaque that says plainly what it is: what comes from a real scan of a real statue, what was made for it here, and whose work and licence it rests on.

The first three stand below. More are in preparation and will be added only when they are finished to the same standard.

  • SocratesMarble · Athens · the philosopher

    Head from a museum scan of the ancient portrait type (SMK, Copenhagen, via Scan the World, CC BY). No likeness of his body survives, so the robed body was made for this statue.

  • ShivaDark bronze · India · the great god in meditation

    A real scan of the modern statue at Murudeshwar, Karnataka, by Boris3D Studio (CC BY 3.0). The dark bronze finish is ours.

  • IsisBlack stone and gold · Egypt and Rome · the throne-bearer

    A modelled rendition built to the tradition, not a scan of an ancient statue. Her face follows a Roman bronze bust of Isis (Mount Holyoke College Art Museum; model by 3DWP, CC BY 4.0).

The statues need a device that can show 3D (WebGL). Each statue page loads about 2 MB.
