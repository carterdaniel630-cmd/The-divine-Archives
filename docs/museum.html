The Museum

Walk the archive as a building. The Nine Ages are wings off a long gallery, each chapter is a room, and the comparative themes stand in the Rotunda. The rooms hold the chapter’s Vault objects, its figures from the Pantheon and its symbol.

Every exhibit opens the archive’s own page, which carries the sources and the evidence verdict. Objects in the cases are generic stand-ins, not replicas, and the figures are shown by their interpretive emblems, not as likenesses.
