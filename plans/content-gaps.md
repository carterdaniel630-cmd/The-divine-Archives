# Content gaps by chapter

Made 2026-10-10 for Carter's content batch (workstream 2). For every chapter on main, a list of
sub-topics a reader would expect: key texts, figures, practices, sacred sites, schisms. Each one was
checked against the chapter's own text (the body above its Sources list), not judged from headings.

- **absent**: not mentioned anywhere in the body.
- **thin**: named once or twice in passing, with no explanation.
- Sub-topics that the chapter already covers properly are left out. The search was by keyword, so a
  topic written about in other words may appear here by mistake. The three false alarms found by
  hand (ch20 Yogācāra, written about as "Mind-Only"; ch64 African-initiated churches; ch80 the
  Ismāʿīlīs) are removed.
- Some gaps belong more naturally to a neighbouring chapter (the Council of Nicaea, for example, sits
  in ch22 Patristic Christianity rather than ch16). Check the neighbour before filling one.

## The top 20, filled in this batch (branch `claude/content-depth`)

Ranked by importance: how many people practise it today, how central it is to the tradition in standard
scholarship, and how odd the chapter reads without it. Each one is a new sourced section in its chapter,
logged in that chapter's source log. Every chapter that changed is set back to pending review.
