# ch70 Pre-Islamic Arabia: the Taymāʾ stele's date (decision for Carter)

**What it says now:** the Louvre (which holds the stele, AO 1505) dates it to the fifth century BCE,
citing Joseph Naveh (1970) for the middle of that century, while Peter Stein (2013) dates it to about
380 BCE. The older phrase "often placed in Nabonidus's century" (the sixth century BCE) was taken out
on 2026-10-05, because no study giving that date could be found.

**What's new:** search results describe a 2023 article, "Babel around Taymāʾ and Dūmat". It presents
new Aramaic graffiti left by officials of Nabonidus, and it re-dates the stele to about 500 BCE on the
evidence of the script. I could confirm the article and its argument from the indexed text. I could
not confirm its author or where it was published: one result points to Michael C. A. Macdonald
(Oxford), another to Peter Stein. The article's page (academia.edu/110122928) is blocked from this
session.
