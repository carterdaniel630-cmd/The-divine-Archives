# Sources — Chapter 2: Egypt in the Bronze Age

Per the project sourcing standard, this citation log records the real, checkable sources behind the chapter's specific claims. It is derived from the Sources section published with the chapter; contested points are flagged as contested in the chapter text, and faith claims are kept distinct from historically attested facts.

*Note on sourcing:* Researched from current reference scholarship and summaries of
primary texts rather than memory alone, per the project standard. Genuinely
contested points — the character of Atenism, any Egyptian influence on Israelite
monotheism, the reliability of Plutarch-derived Osiris narrative, and the aptness
of "resurrection" language — are flagged as disputes rather than resolved.
