# Sources — Chapter 40: The Diaspora Religions

Per the project sourcing standard. The chapter presents the Afro-Atlantic religions as living faiths of healing and community, dismantles the racist "voodoo" caricature as false on the evidence (the "voodoo doll" is a European poppet; the monster-zombie is not the Haitian belief), and flags contested points (Bois Caïman details, Davis's zombification hypothesis, African-vs-Creole origins). Expanded 2026-09 into the five-section template.
