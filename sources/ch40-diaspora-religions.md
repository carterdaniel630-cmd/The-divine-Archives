## Notes
- Not supported: the Hollywood "voodoo" of dolls/zombies/curses — a racist distortion; the real religions emphasize healing, community, ancestors, and moral order.
- Open: exact demographics (long practiced in secret); the African-vs-Creole origins of specific spirits (e.g. the New-World Petwo lwa); internal diversity.
- Directly continues ch33 (African Traditional Religion); draws on Catholic Christianity and (for Santería/Umbanda) the Spiritism of ch36.
