*Note on sourcing:* Researched from standard reference scholarship (Britannica, sourced overviews) and the
mainstream Buddhist-studies scholarship of Williams, Schopen, and Nattier rather than memory alone, per the
project standard. The chapter keeps the tradition's **sacred claims** (the sutras as *buddhavacana*; the
cosmic Buddhas; Amitabha's vow) clearly distinct from the **historical record** (the sutras composed
centuries after the Buddha; the movement's minority monastic origins), notes that "**Hinayana**" is a
pejorative not to be applied to Theravada, corrects the overturned "lay movement" theory, draws the careful
cross-era resonance with the era's other savior religions **without** asserting influence, and reserves the
**Vajrayana/Tantra** and the East Asian schools (Chan/Zen, Pure Land) for their own later chapters. Written
to be educational, and closes the Late Antiquity era of this archive.
