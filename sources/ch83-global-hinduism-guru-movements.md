## Flags for Carter
- **2025 Kumbh death toll (corrected 2026-10-05 on Carter's decision: the chapter now gives at least 30 at the Sangam, the official 37 for the day, and the BBC's at least 82, citing The Wire and The South First above).** Original note: The chapter says the crush on 29 January 2025 "killed at least 30 people by the official count; press investigations using hospital and police records put the toll higher." On 19 February 2025 Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath told the Uttar Pradesh Assembly that 37 people had died that day: 30 at the Sangam crush and 7 in a second incident elsewhere in the mela (The Wire, above). A BBC investigation published in June 2025 identified at least 82 deaths (The South First, above). The chapter's sentence remains literally true for the Sangam crush, but the official total is now 37, and the main press investigation can be named. Possible correction: "killed at least 30 people at the Sangam, and 37 in all that day by the official count; a BBC investigation found at least 82 deaths."
- **International Day of Yoga co-sponsors (minor, no change made).** The chapter says resolution 69/131 had 175 co-sponsoring states. India's Permanent Mission to the UN and several later reports give 177; some reports from the day give 175. Both figures appear in good sources; the chapter's "a record" holds for either.
- **Trinidad arrival figures (minor, no change made).** The chapter gives the *Fatel Razack*'s passengers as 217 after a 96-day voyage. The Pluralism Project's summary gives 225 adult passengers (and other accounts other figures), from embarkation counts versus arrival counts. The total of 147,596 Indians arriving in Trinidad matches.
- **Arya Samaj founding date (corrected 2026-10-06 on Carter's decision).** The chapter said 7 April 1875. Britannica (above) gives 10 April 1875 for the first meeting in Bombay, as do most accounts, including Indian press coverage of the Samaj's 150th anniversary in 2025; a minority of accounts give 7 April. The chapter now gives 10 April and notes the 7 April variant. Checked via search results; Britannica's page to be re-read at review.
