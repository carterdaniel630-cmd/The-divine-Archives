## Notes
- Well-supported: Blombos (~73 ka drawing) and Apollo 11 (~30 ka) artefacts; the range and dating of San rock art; the Bleek and Lloyd archive; the Kalahari healing dance and beliefs as recorded; colonial violence and the |Xam's destruction; deep Khoe-San genetic divergence; the coat of arms; the 2017 San Code.
- Not supported: San as unchanged Stone Age survivals; a single meaning for all San art; the Tsodilo 70,000-year python ritual; geometric motifs as proven universal trance imagery.
- Open: centrality of trance; regional/period variation; meaning of the earliest symbols; projecting 19th/20th-c. testimony into prehistory; shared interpretive authority with San communities.
- Placed in era 01 (Prehistory). Numbered ch66 but written last in Batch III.

## Flags for Carter
- **Tsodilo "python" date (minor, no change made).** The chapter says the claim was "published in 2006". The 2006 claim was a university announcement that was widely reported in the press; the full scholarly statement of the ritual interpretation is Coulson, Staurset and Walker, *PaleoAnthropology* 2011 (cited above). The chapter's sentence is not wrong, but "announced in 2006" would be more exact if you want a correction.
- **Botswana 2006 ruling (minor, no change made).** The chapter says the evicted San and Bakgalagadi "won the right ... to return to their land." The judgment (Sesana, above) held by majority that the refusal of entry without permits was unlawful, but also (2–1) that ending services in 2002 was not unlawful. The chapter's sentence agrees with the first holding; the second is not mentioned.
