# Sources — Chapter 79: The First Christian Kingdoms

Per the project sourcing standard. Independent evidence (coins, inscriptions, alphabets, manuscripts, Rufinus and Athanasius) is separated from the later conversion hagiographies; the Ark at Aksum and the Mtskheta robe are reported as untestable traditions.

## Notes
- Well-supported: royal conversions (Armenia c.301–314, Iberia c.326–337, Aksum c.340–350); Ezana's coins/inscriptions; Frumentius and Athanasius; Mashtots's alphabet (c.405); Avarayr (451); Oriental Orthodox separation; Nine Saints; Garima Gospels radiocarbon; 81-book canon; tabot/Timkat; Lalibela; khachkars.
- Not supported: miraculous conversion details; Ark at Aksum (untestable); Christ's robe at Mtskheta; Edessa c.200.
- Open: exact conversion dates; Georgian alphabet origin; sources of Ethiopian OT practices; reach beyond the courts.
- Placed in era 05 (Late Antiquity).
