## Flags for Carter
- **Garima Gospels: how many, and what dates.** The chapter says the Garima Gospels are "two illustrated gospel books" which radiocarbon tests "placed between about 330 and 650". That matches the first announcement (2010). The fullest study since, McKenzie and Watson, *The Garima Gospels* (Oxford: Manar al-Athar, 2016), treats them as **three** manuscripts (Garima 1, 2 and 3) and, as reported in search results, gives separate radiocarbon ranges of about 390–570 for Garima 2 and about 530–660 for Garima 1, with Garima 2 the older. The chapter's broader claim (fifth to seventh centuries rather than c. 1100, and the older one perhaps the earliest complete illustrated Christian manuscript) agrees with them. The chapter text was not changed. Suggested fix if you agree: "three gospel books, two of them illustrated, ... radiocarbon tests placed them between the late fourth and the seventh centuries".
- **Resolved 2026-10-05 (Carter):** the text now follows McKenzie and Watson (2016): three manuscripts, two illustrated, about 390–570 and 530–660.
