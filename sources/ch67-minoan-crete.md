# Sources — Chapter 67: Minoan Crete

Per the project sourcing standard. Minoan religion has no readable texts of its own (Linear A is undeciphered), so the chapter separates what the sites, objects and Linear B records show from the interpretations built on them, above all Evans's Great Goddess, and flags forgeries and contested finds.

## Notes
- Well-supported: sites, offerings, chronology, imagery, Linear B offering lists, Taweret-to-Genius, Thera c. 1600 BCE, aDNA.
- Not supported: single Great Goddess; peaceful matriarchy; Greek myths as Minoan sources; Thera as cause of the palace collapse or of Atlantis. Boston goddess and similar unprovenanced pieces treated as forgeries per Lapatin.
- Open: Linear A; Anemospilia; ring scenes; lustral basins; palace rule; continuity into the Mycenaean pantheon; the Ring of Minos.
- Placed in era 02 (Bronze Age). Complements ch08 (Early Greece), which covers the Mycenaean mainland.
