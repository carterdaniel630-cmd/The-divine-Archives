## Notes
- Coyolxauhqui Stone opening ties architecture to the Coatepec myth (Templo Mayor = Snake Mountain).
- Not-supported: the 80,400 figure (propaganda), the flat "bloodthirsty" caricature, and the Moctezuma-mistook-Cortés-for-Quetzalcoatl story (likely post-conquest fabrication).
- Genuinely open: precise sacrifice scale; whether Ometeotl "monotheism" is genuine or a modern (León-Portilla) reconstruction.
- Cross-links: Maya (ch29), Inca (ch44); Mesopotamia/Egypt temple-mountains (ch2–3).
