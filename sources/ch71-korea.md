## Notes
- Well-supported: dolmens; Samguk yusa; musok ethnography and its suppression; Buddhist arrival dates; Seokguram/Bulguksa; Wonhyo, Uisang, Jinul; Tripitaka Koreana; Joseon ancestor rites; Hangul and its commentary; lay-founded Catholicism and persecutions; Donghak; census.
- Not supported: Dangun as historical and 2333 BCE; North Korea's Dangun tomb; Ichadon miracles; musok as unchanged prehistoric survival.
- Open: dolmen rites; shamanism's origins; Seokguram's design and alignment; counting multiple belonging; religion's future in South Korea.
- Wonhyo's skull-water episode is told as a story, not as documented fact.
