## Notes
- Well-supported: the Báb and Bábí movement and its suppression (Báb executed 1850); Bahá'u'lláh's life, exiles, and corpus; the doctrines and social teachings; the clergy-less order and the Universal House of Justice (1963, Haifa); the nine-sided temples; the documented, internationally-condemned persecution of Bahá'ís in Iran. Caodaism, Ahmadiyya, and Tenrikyo are documented modern religions.
- Not supported (as fact): the theological claims (the Báb/Bahá'u'lláh as Manifestations; divinely orchestrated progressive revelation) are faith. Progressive revelation is a Bahá'í interpretive framework, NOT neutral history — mainstream history does not treat Krishna/Buddha/Jesus/etc. as one divine succession; flagged as such. Adherent numbers (~5–8 million) are debated estimates.
- Genuinely open: the long-term trajectory of these new world faiths; whether they fulfill their universalizing aims; the inner truth of their revelations (documented as belief, not adjudicated).
- Placed in era 09 (Modern). Re-scoped from an earlier "Rastafari" idea to avoid overlap with ch40 (which covers Rastafari); Rastafari and other NRMs are noted as belonging to ch35/ch40.
- Closing chapter of the Nine Ages: fittingly, the tradition that most explicitly tries to unite everything the archive has surveyed.
- Connects to ch21 (Shia Islam roots), ch55 (Manichaean prophetic succession), ch37 (Theosophy/perennialism), ch35 (NRMs), ch10/ch21 (abjad/numerology).
