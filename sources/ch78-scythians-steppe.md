## Notes
- Well-supported: steppe nomad cultures and kurgans; Herodotus's text; archaeological confirmation of funeral elements; Pazyryk/Ice Maiden; hemp equipment and Jirzankal cannabis residues; animal style; Arzhan; women buried with weapons; Ossetian descent.
- Not supported: every Herodotean detail; Amazons as a single nation of women; simple Scythian ancestry of modern nations.
- Open: meaning of animal style; accuracy of Greek god equivalents; three-function reading; Enarees; meaning of hemp rites and tattoos; ancient content of Nart sagas.
- Placed in era 04 (Axial Age), Herodotus's time; covers Arzhan (9th–7th c. BCE) to the Sarmatians.
