# Sources — Vault V72: The Book of Shadows

Per the project sourcing standard, this citation log records the real, checkable sources behind the entry's specific claims. It is derived from the Sources section published with the entry. Contested points are flagged as contested in the entry text.

**Drafting note (2026-09-27, batch 8).** Facts were checked by web search during drafting. The drafting environment could not open most holding institutions' sites directly because its network policy blocked them; institutional URLs should be re-checked in review. No institutional photographs are reproduced, and for secret-sacred or devotional objects (the tjurunga, the Báb) no image of any kind is shown. The interactive objects present documented facts and primary text, in public-domain originals or this archive's own working renderings; schematic objects are labelled as such.

**Source revision (2026-10-17, build loop).** The four Wikipedia citations were replaced with sources opened and
checked on 2026-10-17: the World Religions and Spirituality Project profile (confirms the 1953 initiation, the revised
texts and the rewritten Charge), the Open University OpenLearn course (confirms Valiente's development of Gardner's
rituals and the Charge), and the Doreen Valiente Foundation's "About" page (confirms 1953 and the Charge in the early
Books of Shadows). None of these three pages mentions the Wiccan Rede. The entry's 1964 Rede line rests on Hutton
(1999, already cited; the book was not re-opened in this session) and on Doyle White's article, whose Project MUSE page
could not be opened from the build environment (access check): its title and address were confirmed by search only,
so its volume and page numbers are not given. Re-check both in review.

## Sources cited

*The book and its makers*

- V. Crowley, "Doreen Valiente", World Religions and Spirituality Project (2018), https://wrldrels.org/?p=14714 (her 1953 initiation by Gardner; her revision of the coven's texts, which reduced the reliance on Crowley; her rewrite of the Charge)
- The Open University, *A spiritual revolution? Wicca and religious change in the 1960s*, §3.1 "Gardner's coven", OpenLearn, https://www.open.edu/openlearn/history-the-arts/a-spiritual-revolution-wicca-and-religious-change-the-1960s/content-section-3.1 (Gardner's coven; Valiente's development of the rituals; the Charge of the Goddess)
- The Doreen Valiente Foundation, "About", https://doreenvaliente.org/about-us (1953 initiation; the Charge of the Goddess incorporated into the early Gardnerian Books of Shadows)
- E. Doyle White, "'An' it Harm None, Do What Ye Will': A Historical Analysis of the Wiccan Rede", Project MUSE, https://muse.jhu.edu/article/609097 (the Rede's history; see the source log)
