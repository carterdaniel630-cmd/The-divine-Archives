# Sources — Chapter 37: Theosophy and the Occult Revival

Per the project sourcing standard and CLAUDE.md's esoterica brief (Hermeticism, Golden Dawn, Thelema). The chapter separates the movements' documented history and huge influence from their unverifiable claims, and flags the root-race doctrine's later racist misuse.

## Sources cited
- **Blavatsky; the Theosophical Society (1875); synthesis of Eastern religion and Western occultism; New Age influence** — "Theosophy," Britannica. https://www.britannica.com/topic/theosophy
- **The Secret Doctrine (1888); rounds and root races; Lemuria and Atlantis** — "Root race," Wikipedia; "The Secret Doctrine," Wikipedia. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Root_race
- **The Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn (1888); Hermeticism/Kabbalah/tarot/ceremonial magic; influence on Wicca and Thelema** — Yale University Press, "The Golden Dawn and the Origins of Wicca." https://yalebooks.yale.edu/2023/10/31/the-hermetic-order-of-the-golden-dawn-and-the-origins-of-wicca/
- **Theosophy and the founding of the New Age movement** — "New Age," Wikipedia. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Age
- **The 1885 SPR (Hodgson) report judging the Mahatma letters fraudulent; plagiarism charges** — "Helena Blavatsky," Wikipedia. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helena_Blavatsky

## Notes
- Not supported: the hidden Masters/materializing letters (SPR: fraud); root races and lost continents; Blavatsky's claimed ancient sources (recombined from contemporary books).
- Symbology: the tarot–Tree of Life mapping is real but the "Egyptian" tarot pedigree is an 18th-c. invention.
- Warning flagged: the root-race scheme was bent toward racism and fed proto-Nazi occultism, even as Theosophy preached universal brotherhood.
