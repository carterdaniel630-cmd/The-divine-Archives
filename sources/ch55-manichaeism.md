## Notes
- Well-supported: Mani's historicity and dates; the two-principle dualism and Light/Darkness cosmology; the Elect/Hearer structure and ascetic ethic; Mani's self-written canon and picture-book; the vast spread and Uyghur adoption; Augustine's involvement; the near-total suppression; the recovered texts.
- Not supported (as fact): much of the content of Mani's own scriptures (lost; known via fragments and hostile summaries — reconstructions provisional); a direct genealogical descent to the Cathars/Bogomils (contested; most scholars see typological resemblance, not unbroken transmission).
- Genuinely open: precise original cosmology/liturgy; how much of Mani's self-presentation reflects his actual teaching vs. later elaboration; the exact channels/degree of influence on later dualist movements.
- Placed in era 05 (Late Antiquity), Mani's 3rd-century floruit.
