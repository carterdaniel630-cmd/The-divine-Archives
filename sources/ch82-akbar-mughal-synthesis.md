# Sources — Chapter 82: Akbar & the Mughal Synthesis

Per the project sourcing standard. The chapter weighs its three interested witnesses (Abu'l-Fazl, Badauni, the Jesuits) against each other, reports the debate over what the Din-i Ilahi was without resolving it, keeps Sikh and Jain devotional traditions distinct from documentary record, and presents the Aurangzeb temple debate from both sides.
