# Sources — Chapter 42: The Neolithic

Per the project sourcing standard, and the rule that uncertain prehistory stays honest rather than padded. The chapter keeps the (abundant) architecture separate from its (unknown) theology, and gives a clear not-supported verdict on the Mellaart/Gimbutas universal Mother Goddess. Expanded 2026-09 into the five-section template (with a reconstructive believer's lens).

## Sources cited
- **Göbekli Tepe (~9600 BCE), T-pillars, animal reliefs, Schmidt's "temple first" hypothesis** — Biblical Archaeology Society; German Archaeological Institute, "The Tepe Telegrams." https://www.biblicalarchaeology.org/daily/ancient-cultures/ancient-near-eastern-world/the-gobekli-tepe-ruins-and-the-origins-of-neolithic-religion/
- **Karahan Tepe / Taş Tepeler; skull-cult evidence; Banning's critique of the "cult centre" reading** — "Paradise Found or Common Sense Lost?", Open Archaeology (2022). https://www.degruyterbrill.com/document/doi/10.1515/opar-2022-0317/html
- **Jericho and 'Ain Ghazal plastered skulls (PPNB); 'Ain Ghazal plaster statues** — D. Schmandt-Besserat, "The Plastered Skulls" (UT Austin). https://sites.utexas.edu/dsb/ain-ghazal/the-plastered-skulls/
- **Çatalhöyük: under-floor burial, bucrania, "history houses," reassessment of the goddess theory (Hodder; Meskell/Nakamura)** — Çatalhöyük Research Project, "Was there a belief in the Mother Goddess…?" http://www.catalhoyuk.com/node/736
- **Mother Goddess hypothesis (Mellaart, Gimbutas) and its critique** — "Mellaart, Gimbutas, Goddesses, and Çatalhöyük." https://www.academia.edu/71690356/MELLAART_GIMBUTAS_GODDESSES_AND_%C3%87ATALH%C3%96Y%C3%9CK
- **Megaliths and solstice alignments (Stonehenge/Durrington, Newgrange, Malta Ġgantija/Hypogeum)** — "Megalith," Wikipedia. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megalith
- **Ness of Brodgar and the Heart of Neolithic Orkney** — The Ness of Brodgar Project. https://www.nessofbrodgar.co.uk/
