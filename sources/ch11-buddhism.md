# Sources — Chapter 11: Buddhism: The Religion of No-Self

Per the project sourcing standard, this citation log records the real, checkable sources behind the chapter's specific claims. It is derived from the Sources section published with the chapter; contested points are flagged as contested in the chapter text, and faith claims are kept distinct from historically attested facts.

*Note on sourcing:* Researched from standard scholarship (Gombrich and reference works)
and summaries of the early texts rather than memory alone, per the project standard.
The chapter distinguishes the **historical** core (a śramaṇa teacher; the early
doctrine; Ashoka's evidenced patronage) from the **legendary** biography, flags the
disputed dates and the debated meaning of nirvana/anatta, and reserves the later
Mahayana developments and the Asian spread for subsequent chapters. Written to be
educational, with particular attention to the contrast with the Vedic/Upanishadic
world of Chapter 5.
