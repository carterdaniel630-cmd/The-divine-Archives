## The museum's "Read it in English" panel (added 2026-10-04)

The panel gives a **summary in our own words**, not a translation: the one full English rendering (O. Aqiqi and
T. Lawson, "Haykal or Star Tablet Written in the Báb's Own Hand: Form, Content, and Provisional Translation",
https://bahai-library.com/aqiqi_lawson_bab_haykal) is in copyright. bahai-library.com cannot be opened from the
drafting environment, so the summary's points (the five-pointed haykal; verses of the Báb's own composition in a
Qur'anic style, not quoted from the Qur'an; about 360 derivatives of *bahá'*) were confirmed through search results
and through an accessible reprint: "The Star Tablet of the Bab", The Good Men Project,
https://goodmenproject.com/education-2/the-star-tablet-of-the-bab/ . To re-read against Momen and Aqiqi & Lawson at
review. No image of the Báb is shown anywhere.
