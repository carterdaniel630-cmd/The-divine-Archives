*Note on sourcing:* Researched from standard reference scholarship (Internet Encyclopedia of
Philosophy, Britannica, Wikipedia's sourced overviews, and the mainstream scholarship of Ehrman,
Williams, King, and Pagels they summarize) rather than memory alone, per the project standard. The
chapter foregrounds the crucial shift from knowing the Gnostics only through **hostile
heresiologists** to reading their **own words** at Nag Hammadi, flags the live debate over whether
**"Gnosticism" is even a coherent category**, corrects the heresiologists' likely-slanderous
libertine accusations against the Gnostics' own mostly-ascetic texts, keeps the well-attested
teachings separate from the contested origins and the debated dating of the **Gospel of Thomas**,
and reserves the **Roman mystery cults** and **Manichaeism's** fuller treatment for their own
places. Written to be educational, and to let a much-maligned family of traditions be understood on
its own terms as well as its critics'.
