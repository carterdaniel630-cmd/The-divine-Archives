## Notes
- Well-supported: succession dispute; Ghadir hadith in both traditions; Ali's caliphate and death; Karbala 680; branches; Fatimids and Cairo 969; occultation doctrine; Usuli/Akhbari and marja'; Muharram rites and pilgrimages; Safavid 1501; velayat-e faqih; population range.
- Not supported: existence of the twelfth Imam (faith); Imams' miracles; taqiyya as general deception; unchanging 1,400-year conflict; precise pilgrimage counts.
- Open: meaning of Ghadir; early Karbala narratives; development of sinlessness doctrine; Alawite/Alevi relations; future of clerical authority.
- Placed in era 06 (Early Medieval), where its founding events fall; runs to the present.
