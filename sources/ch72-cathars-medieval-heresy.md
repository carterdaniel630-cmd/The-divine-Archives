## Notes
- Well-supported: dissenting dualist groups attested in their own texts; consolamentum and perfects; the crusade, Béziers, burnings; the inquisition and Fournier register; Montségur; Waldensians, Porete, Wyclif, Hus.
- Not supported: Arnaud's 'Kill them all' (second-hand, decades later); Cathar Grail/treasure/bloodline; today's castles as Cathar buildings; dove/Occitan cross as Cathar symbols; Free Spirit as a proven sect.
- Open: how organized/uniform the Cathar church was; Bogomil links and Saint-Félix; the endura; numbers.
- Placed in era 07 (High Medieval). Complements ch17 and ch55 on dualism and ch32 on persecution.

## Wikipedia replacement (2026-10-05)
- Eighteen of the twenty-five Wikipedia citations were replaced with scholarly or primary sources supporting the same claims: Barber, *The Cathars* (2nd ed., 2013), on the Cathars, Montségur, Saint-Félix and the "Kill them all" phrase; Wakefield & Evans, *Heresies of the High Middle Ages* (1969), for the Cathar texts in translation; Peter of les Vaux-de-Cernay's *Historia Albigensis* (trans. Sibly, 1998); Caesarius of Heisterbach's *Dialogue on Miracles* (trans. 1929); Given, *Inquisition and Medieval Society* (1997), and canon 3 of the Fourth Lateran Council; Duvernoy's edition of the Fournier register (1965) and Le Roy Ladurie's *Montaillou* (1975); Cameron, *Waldenses* (2000); Field (2012) on Porete; Lerner (1972) on the Free Spirit.
- Seven were at first kept as labelled pointers. In a follow-up pass (2026-10-05) six of them were replaced: Peters, *Heresy and Authority in Medieval Europe* (1980), for *Ad abolendam*; Burr, *The Spiritual Franciscans* (2001), and Douie, *The Heresy of the Fraticelli* (1932); Hudson, *The Premature Reformation* (1988), for the Lollards; Fudge, *Jan Hus* (2010); and the UNESCO Tentative List entry for Carcassonne and its mountain "sentinel" castles (2017), for the "Cathar castles" as royal French fortresses. For Otto Rahn, his own book (*Crusade Against the Grail*, English translation 2006, German original 1933) now documents his Grail claim; "Otto Rahn" on Wikipedia is kept as a labelled pointer only for his SS membership, because no stronger source for that detail was confirmed in this pass.
- Bibliographic details were confirmed via search results; publisher pages are to be re-read at review. Field (2012) confirms the dates of Porete's trial: sentenced 31 May 1310 and burned the next day, which matches the chapter's 1 June 1310. No sentence of the chapter was changed.
