## Notes
- Well-supported: size and growth of the unaffiliated (Pew); spiritual beliefs among nones; ancient and Enlightenment doubt; French revolutionary cults; church–state separation; freethought; state atheism and its persecutions; humanist organizations; secularization theories and critics.
- Not supported: the strong secularization thesis; nonbelievers as less moral; cognitive science as proof or disproof of religion; God's existence or non-existence (not established either way).
- Open: future of nonreligion in the West and globally (demography); measuring belonging/believing; explanatory reach of cognitive science of religion; secular societies' long-run sources of community and meaning.
