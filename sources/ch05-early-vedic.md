# Sources — Chapter 5: The Early Vedic World in the Bronze Age

Per the project sourcing standard, this citation log records the real, checkable sources behind the chapter's specific claims. It is derived from the Sources section published with the chapter; contested points are flagged as contested in the chapter text, and faith claims are kept distinct from historically attested facts.

## Sources cited

*Primary texts (translations)*
- *The Rigveda*, trans. Stephanie Jamison &amp; Joel Brereton (2014), the current
  scholarly standard; Wendy Doniger, *The Rig Veda: An Anthology* (Penguin).
- Creation hymns: [Wikipedia: Nasadiya Sukta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nasadiya_Sukta);
  [Hiranyagarbha Sukta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiranyagarbha_S%C5%ABkta);
  Purusha Sukta (Rigveda 10.90).
- The Gayatri Mantra (Rigveda 3.62.10) — [Wikipedia: Gayatri Mantra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gayatri_Mantra).

*Deities, Rta, and the pantheon*
- [Britannica: Vedic religion](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Vedic-religion);
  [Wikipedia: Historical Vedic religion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Historical_Vedic_religion);
  Mitra–Varuna and Rta overviews.

*Ritual, texts, and transmission*
- Structure of the Vedas (Samhita/Brahmana/Aranyaka/Upanishad) and the *shakhas*;
  the srauta priesthood and soma sacrifice — standard reference overviews.
- Frits Staal, *Agni: The Vedic Ritual of the Fire Altar* (on the Agnicayana and oral
  ritual), for the fire-altar symbolism.

*Comparative and Indo-Iranian*
- On soma/haoma and the plant debate: [Wikipedia: Botanical identity of soma–haoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Botanical_identity_of_soma%E2%80%93haoma).
- Indo-European poetics and theonyms (Dyaus/Zeus/Jupiter): M. L. West, *Indo-European
  Poetry and Myth* (2007); Calvert Watkins, *How to Kill a Dragon* (1995).

*On dating and the Indo-Aryan question*
- Ancient DNA: V. Shinde et al., *Cell* (2019); V. Narasimhan et al., *Science* (2019).

*Note on sourcing:* Researched from current scholarship and standard translations
rather than memory alone, per the project standard. The genuinely contested points —
the date of the Rigveda, the interpolation status of the varna verse, the identity of
soma, and the Indus/Indo-Aryan relationship — are flagged as disputes rather than
resolved, and the politically charged questions are reported, not adjudicated.
