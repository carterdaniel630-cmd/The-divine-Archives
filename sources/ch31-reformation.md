# Sources — Chapter 31: The Reformation

Per the project sourcing standard, this citation log records the real, checkable sources behind the chapter's claims. The events and texts are abundantly documented; the chapter keeps them distinct from the theological claims (recorded as faith, not adjudicated) and flags the genuinely debated points (the door legend, the Weber thesis).

## Notes on handling
- Theological claims (justification by faith, sola scriptura, predestination, real presence) are recorded as **faith**, neither proven nor refuted.
- The **door legend** (whether Luther physically nailed the Theses up) and the **Weber "Protestant ethic → capitalism" thesis** are flagged as debated.
- The witch trials of the same era are treated in their own chapter (ch32) as documented persecution, not validation of the accusations.
- Sikhism (ch34) is noted as a parallel vernacular scripture-and-devotion movement of the same century.
