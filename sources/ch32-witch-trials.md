# Sources — Chapter 32: The Witch Trials

Per the project sourcing standard, and CLAUDE.md's mandate to treat the witch-trial era as documented history/persecution, not validation of trial-era accusations. The chapter holds steady that the persecution was real and the satanic conspiracy imaginary; the Murray witch-cult thesis and the "nine million" toll are named as discredited/mythical. Expanded 2026-09 into the five-section template (with the believer's lens = the persecutors' worldview).
