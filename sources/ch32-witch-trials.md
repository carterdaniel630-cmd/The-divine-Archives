# Sources — Chapter 32: The Witch Trials

Per the project sourcing standard and CLAUDE.md's explicit rule, this chapter treats the witch trials as **documented history/persecution, not as validation of trial-era accusations**. The citation log keeps the real (the trials, the numbers, folk magic, the persecution) distinct from the imaginary (the diabolical witch-cult) and flags the debunked myths.

## Sources cited
- **Chronology (c. 1450–1750); ~100,000 accused, 40,000–60,000 executed; concentration in the Holy Roman Empire** — "The European Witch-Hunts, 1450–1750," Brewminate. https://brewminate.com/the-european-witch-hunts-1450-1750/
- **The "cumulative concept" (maleficium + demonology); multipolar causes (Levack); inquisitorial procedure and torture** — EBSCO Research Starters, "Witch-Hunts and Witch Trials." https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/history/witch-hunts-and-witch-trials
- **Malleus Maleficarum (1486, Heinrich Kramer); best-seller yet never Church-authorized and condemned by theologians** — "Malleus Maleficarum," Wikipedia. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malleus_Maleficarum
- **Gender: ~75–80% women, with male-majority regions (Iceland ~92%, Finland, Estonia)** — Gendercide, "Witch-hunts in early modern Europe"; Grokipedia, "Witch trials." https://www.gendercide.org/case_witchhunts.html
- **Salem (1692, 20 executed); Friedrich Spee's Cautio Criminalis (1631); the end of the trials** — "Witches: Real Origins, Hunts & Trials," History.com. https://www.history.com/articles/history-of-witches
- **The debunked Margaret Murray "witch-cult" thesis; the mythical "nine million" toll** — Grokipedia, "Witch trials in the early modern period." https://grokipedia.com/page/Witch_trials_in_the_early_modern_period

## Notes on handling
- **Not supported:** that the accused were an actual satanic witch-cult (Murray thesis, debunked); that torture-confessions describe real events; that maleficium worked. The crime as charged was imaginary; the persecution real.
- Real village **cunning-folk / folk magic** existed and were mostly benign — recast as diabolism by the persecutors.
- Death tolls are estimates; the "nine million" figure is a myth.
- The chapter explicitly links forward to the modern **Satanism** chapter (fabricated accusatory "Satanism" vs. real modern self-identified Satanism) and to modern **Wicca**'s mythologized descent — both treated in era 09.
