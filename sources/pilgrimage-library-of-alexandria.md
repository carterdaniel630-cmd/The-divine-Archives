# Sources: the Pilgrimage, the Library of Alexandria (reconstruction)

Model: `docs/pilgrimage/sites/library-of-alexandria/` (label **(R)**, status **preview**: built, noindex, off the
menu). Sizes in `dims.js` are all CONJECTURE; the info-point texts are in `site.js`. Logged 2026-10-10.

**How the facts were checked:** through search results quoting or summarising each source; the sources' own pages
could not be opened from this session and are to be re-read at review.

## What the reconstruction rests on
- **Strabo, *Geography* 17.1.8** (trans. H. L. Jones, Loeb Classical Library, 1932): the Museum "has a public
  walk, an exedra with seats, and a large house, in which is the common mess-hall of the men of learning", with a
  priest once appointed by the kings and now by Caesar. Translation quoted on the University of Texas at
  Arlington reading list, https://websites.uta.edu/hunnicut/reading-list/readings-western-civilization/strabo-description-of-alexandria ;
  UCL Digital Egypt, https://www.ucl.ac.uk/museums-static/digitalegypt/museum/museum3.html
- Everything else about the buildings (plan, sizes, columns, garden, the book room's niches after later Roman
  libraries) is our conjecture, said so on every card.

## Facts used, by info point
- **Holdings 40,000–700,000 rolls unreliable; the modest real scale of Greek literature**: R. S. Bagnall,
  "Alexandria: Library of Dreams", *Proceedings of the American Philosophical Society* 146, no. 4 (2002):
  348–362, https://archive.nyu.edu/handle/2451/28263 ; The Open University, OpenLearn, *The Library of
  Alexandria*, "Assessing the evidence", https://www.open.edu/openlearn/history-the-arts/library-alexandria/content-section-2.1
- **Callimachus and the Pinakes (120 books); the librarians Zenodotus, Apollonius, Eratosthenes, Aristophanes;
  Callimachus not head librarian**: World History Encyclopedia, "Callimachus of Cyrene",
  https://www.worldhistory.org/Callimachus_of_Cyrene/
- **The Letter of Aristeas** (Demetrius of Phalerum; seventy-two translators; dated mid-2nd century BCE or later;
  Demetrius an adviser of Ptolemy I, banished by Ptolemy II): *Jewish Encyclopedia* (1906), "Aristeas, Letter
  of", https://www.jewishencyclopedia.com/articles/1765-aristeas-letter-of ; H. St J. Thackeray (trans.), *The
  Letter of Aristeas* (1917), https://www.gospelstudies.org.uk/earlychurch/pdf/e-books/thackeray_h-st-j/letter-of-aristeas.pdf
- **How it ended** (Caesar 48 BCE and the conflicting accounts of Plutarch and Florus/Lucan; Aurelian in the 270s;
  the Serapeum 391; the late Umar story rejected by most historians): *National Geographic*, "Who burned the
  Library of Alexandria?", https://www.nationalgeographic.com/history/article/who-burned-library-of-alexandria-egypt ;
  Bagnall (2002).
- **Site unknown; the Bibliotheca Alexandrina near the old royal quarter**: Bibliotheca Alexandrina,
  https://www.bibalex.org/libraries/Presentation/Static/15612.aspx ; UNC pathfinder "The Ancient Library of
  Alexandria: geography", https://ils.unc.edu/dpr/path/alexandria/geography.htm
