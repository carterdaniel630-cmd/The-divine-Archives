# Sources — Chapter 28: Scholasticism

Per the project sourcing standard. Records what scholasticism did and how, without certifying that its arguments (the Five Ways, the ontological argument) succeed; corrects the "Dark Ages" myth without overclaiming that scholasticism invented modern science. Expanded 2026-09 into the five-section template.
