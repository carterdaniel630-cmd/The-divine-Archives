# Sources — Chapter 75: Siberian & Arctic Shamanism

Per the project sourcing standard. The chapter separates the ethnographic record (seventeenth to twentieth centuries) from the claim that shamanism is humanity's oldest religion, reports archaeologists' 'shaman burial' identifications as interpretations, and flags Eliade's universal category and Wasson's Soma theory as contested.
