## Museum rendition (visual pass, 2026-10-06)

`docs/museum/relics.js`, `CAT.v43`:
- **Dimensions:** 3.18 × 1.63 m, as most commonly reported. Some sources give 3.10 × 1.60 m (e.g. https://egypt-museum.com/victory-stele-of-merneptah/); the label says so. The thickness is not given in the sources found; it is modelled at 0.30 m.
- **Material:** black granite.
- **Layout:** a lunette scene over 28 lines of text. The marks are illustrative; no reliable public-domain Unicode transcription of the hieroglyphic text exists to copy.
- **The back:** the stela was first inscribed for Amenhotep III and reused by Merneptah; the back of the rendition carries illustrative marks for that earlier inscription (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merneptah_Stele, to be replaced with a scholarly source at review).
- **Not matched to photographs here:** the holder's images could not be opened from the build environment.
