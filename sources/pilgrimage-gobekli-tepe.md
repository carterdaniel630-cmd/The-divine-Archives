# Sources: the Pilgrimage, Göbekli Tepe

Model: `docs/pilgrimage/sites/gobekli-tepe/` (status **preview**: built, noindex, off the menu). Sizes are in
`dims.js`, each marked SURVEY (a published figure) or APPROX; pillar positions are in `layout.js`; the
info-point texts are in `site.js`; the carvings are drawn in `pillars.js`. Logged 2026-10-11.

**How the facts were checked:** this session could not open most excavation reports or publisher pages. Each
fact below was confirmed through search results that quote or summarise the source, or through the excavators'
own blog, and is to be re-read against the source itself at review. No measured plan could be opened, so **the
layout (where each enclosure lies, the place of every pillar, the wall shapes) is an estimate** after published
plans, not a measurement. The carvings are our own simplified renderings, not tracings.

## Notes for review
- **ch42 and the fill:** ch42 says the enclosures were deliberately backfilled. The excavators have argued since
  2019 that the fill slid in from the slopes (Clare 2020). The "Buried on purpose?" card says the question is now
  contested and that ch42 still states the older reading; ch42 may need the same flag.
- Pillar 43's position in the north-west of Enclosure D's wall follows published plans; its place here is
  approximate. Which animal is on which other pillar is illustrative, as the cards say.
- **Review fixes (2026-10-12):** the 1963 survey and Schmidt's 1994 visit now cite the DAI post "Part 1: A (Re-) Discovery (1994–1996)", https://www.dainst.blog/the-tepe-telegrams/?p=861 (read by the reviewer; blocked from this session); the Vulture Stone card now says "Egyptian, Arabian and Greek scholars", as the excavators' reply does.
