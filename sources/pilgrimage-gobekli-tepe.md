# Sources: the Pilgrimage, Göbekli Tepe

Model: `docs/pilgrimage/sites/gobekli-tepe/` (status **preview**: built, noindex, off the menu). Sizes are in
`dims.js`, each marked SURVEY (a published figure) or APPROX; pillar positions are in `layout.js`; the
info-point texts are in `site.js`; the carvings are drawn in `pillars.js`. Logged 2026-10-11.

**How the facts were checked:** this session could not open most excavation reports or publisher pages. Each
fact below was confirmed through search results that quote or summarise the source, or through the excavators'
own blog, and is to be re-read against the source itself at review. No measured plan could be opened, so **the
layout (where each enclosure lies, the place of every pillar, the wall shapes) is an estimate** after published
plans, not a measurement. The carvings are our own simplified renderings, not tracings.

## Excavators' publications
- L. Clare, "Göbekli Tepe, Turkey. A brief summary of research at a new World Heritage Site (2015–2019)",
  *e-Forschungsberichte des DAI* 2020.2, 81–88: https://publications.dainst.org/journals/efb/article/view/2596
  (dates c. 9500–8000 BCE; dwellings, grinding tools, cistern and channels; revision of the "temple" reading;
  the fill as slope slide rather than deliberate backfill; the modern shelter and walkways).
- German Archaeological Institute (DAI), *The Tepe Telegrams* (the project blog): https://www.dainst.blog/the-tepe-telegrams/
  - J. Notroff and others, "How did they do it? Making and moving monoliths at Göbekli Tepe" (May 2016); posts
    tagged "quarries": https://www.dainst.blog/the-tepe-telegrams/tag/quarries (unfinished pillar, about 7 m;
    flint tools; weights of the central pillars). **Re-check:** the exact post URL and the 50-tonne figure.
  - J. Notroff, "Archaeoastronomy, meteor showers, mass extinction: What does the fox say?" (21 April 2017):
    https://www.dainst.blog/the-tepe-telegrams/2017/04/21/archaeoastronomy-meteor-showers-mass-extinction-what-does-the-fox-say-and-what-the-crane-the-aurochs/
    (reply to Sweatman and Tsikritsis; the roofing argument against sky-watching).
  - J. Notroff, "A Sanctuary … or so fair a House?" (24 January 2017) (the temple-or-house debate).
- J. Notroff, O. Dietrich, L. Clare and others, "More than a vulture: a response to Sweatman and Tsikritsis",
  *Mediterranean Archaeology and Archaeometry* 17.2 (2017), 57–63.
- M. Kinzel and L. Clare, "Monumental – compared to what? A perspective from Göbekli Tepe", in A. B. Gebauer and
  others (eds), *Monumentalising Life in the Neolithic* (Oxbow, 2020) (likely roofing).
- *Current World Archaeology* 53 (2012), "Turkey: Göbekli Tepe" (Enclosures A–D, pillar counts, Pillar 27, the
  central pillars of C on pedestals).

## Notes for review
- **ch42 and the fill:** ch42 says the enclosures were deliberately backfilled. The excavators have argued since
  2019 that the fill slid in from the slopes (Clare 2020). The "Buried on purpose?" card says the question is now
  contested and that ch42 still states the older reading; ch42 may need the same flag.
- Pillar 43's position in the north-west of Enclosure D's wall follows published plans; its place here is
  approximate. Which animal is on which other pillar is illustrative, as the cards say.
