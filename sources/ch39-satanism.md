# Sources — Chapter 39: Satanism

Per the project sourcing standard and CLAUDE.md's explicit brief: cover (a) fabricated accusatory "Satanism" and (b) real modern self-identified Satanism, with descriptive, non-sensationalized rigor, treated like any other tradition.

## Sources cited
- **Church of Satan (1966); Anton LaVey; atheistic LaVeyan Satanism (Satan as symbol, not deity)** — "The Satanic Bible," Britannica; "LaVeyan Satanism," Wikipedia. https://www.britannica.com/topic/The-Satanic-Bible
- **The Satanic Bible (1969); LaVey's ethical egoism** — "Anton LaVey," Britannica. https://www.britannica.com/biography/Anton-LaVey
- **The Church of Satan's own statement of atheistic beliefs** — Church of Satan, "F.A.Q. Fundamental Beliefs." https://churchofsatan.com/faq-fundamental-beliefs/
- **The Satanic Temple (2013); non-theism; Seven Tenets; church-state activism; 2019 recognition** — "The Satanic Temple," Wikipedia. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Satanic_Temple
- **The Satanic Panic; the FBI's 1992 Lanning report finding no evidence of ritual-abuse networks** — "Satanic panic," Wikipedia. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satanic_panic
- **Baphomet as Éliphas Lévi's 1856 creation (not an ancient demon); the Black Mass as largely literary** — "Baphomet," Wikipedia. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baphomet

## Notes
- The two Satanisms are kept strictly separate: the imagined/accusatory Devil-cult (no members; the scapegoat fantasy of the witch trials and the Satanic Panic) vs. real, mostly atheistic self-identified Satanism (Satan as symbol).
- Not supported: any vast network of murderous Devil-worshippers (Lanning report: essentially no basis).
- Theistic Satanism / Luciferianism / the neo-Nazi Order of Nine Angles named honestly as a small, fringe (and, in the last case, dangerous) minority.
