# Sources — Chapter 39: Satanism

Per the project sourcing standard and the CLAUDE.md mandate to treat Satanism descriptively and non-sensationally. The chapter separates accusatory/imaginary Satanism (a persecutors' fabrication) from self-identified Satanism (mostly atheistic), documents the panic's emptiness (Lanning), and names the theistic and criminal fringe honestly without reviving the conspiracy myth. Expanded 2026-09 into the five-section template.
