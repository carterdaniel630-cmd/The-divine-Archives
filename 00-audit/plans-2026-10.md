# Plans (not built) — 2026-10

Two plans Carter asked for: a first-screen redesign, and cutting the fighter's 6.2 MB download.
Nothing here has been built. Each option says what changes visually, the effort, and the trade-off.

---

## 1. First-screen redesign (home page)

### What's there now

On a phone, the first screen (`docs/index.html`, the `.masthead`, set to 92% of the screen
height) shows:
- the compass emblem;
- **THE DIVINE ARCHIVES**;
- one italic sentence;
- a search box;
- a faint "Descend through the ages" hint.

There's no menu and nothing else to tap. The eight ways in (Era, Tradition, Themes, Symbols,
Vault, Pantheon, Museum, Arcade) start below the fold, each one a full screen tall. The best
hooks (Vault, Museum, games) are the 5th, 7th and 8th panels.

### Options

**A. A "doorways" strip under the search box** *(recommended first)*
- **What changes visually:** the first screen keeps its look (emblem, title, sentence,
  search). Under the search box sits one row of 4–5 small gold-outlined buttons: *The Nine
  Ages · The Vault · The Museum · Play · A chapter at random*. On a phone they wrap onto two
  short lines. The emblem shrinks a little (108 → about 80 px) so everything fits above the fold
  on a typical phone.
- **What it fixes:** a newcomer sees what's inside without scrolling, and the three strongest
  hooks are one tap away.
- **Effort:** small, about 1–2 hours. One block of HTML and CSS on the home page, no new
  pages, and nothing to rebuild.
- **Trade-off:** the first screen loses a little of its empty, solemn feel.

**B. A short masthead with three "featured" cards**
- **What changes visually:** the title block shrinks to about half the screen. Below it, three
  cards can be swiped sideways:
  1. **From the Vault** (one object with its emblem and one line, for example "The Shroud of
     Turin: a cloth that will not settle");
  2. **Walk the Museum**;
  3. **Play the Archive** (a game's icon).

  The cards could rotate on each visit.
- **What it fixes:** the first screen shows content, not just a title, and it changes, which
  rewards return visits.
- **Effort:** medium, about half a day to a day. It needs the cards, the swipe row, a small
  picker for the featured object, and checks on small phones.
- **Trade-off:** more moving parts. The featured object needs care so a sensitive object (the
  Black Stone, the tjurunga) is never used as decoration.

**C. A proper site menu on every page, including home**
- **What changes visually:** home gets the same top bar as inner pages. On phones, the
  two-line, 8-link bar on every page becomes the site name plus a **Menu** button that opens a
  full-screen list.
- **What it fixes:**
  - the home page's missing menu;
  - the inner pages' header taking about a fifth of the screen;
  - the chapter title appearing only at the bottom of the first screen.
- **Effort:** medium, about a day. It touches the header in the three build templates and the
  hand-written pages, and needs a keyboard-accessible menu.
- **Trade-off:** a site-wide change, so it needs the most testing.

**D. A full "start here" home**
- **What changes visually:** the archive's numbers up front (83 chapters · 88 objects · 199
  figures · 12 games), a "where to begin" guide by interest ("I like mysteries" → Vault, "I like
  history" → Eras, "I want to play" → Arcade), and a featured chapter.
- **Effort:** large, a few days, plus your review of the words.
- **Trade-off:** it's a new home page rather than a fix to the current one.

**Recommendation:** **A now** (cheap, and it fixes the biggest first-impression problem), then
**C** (it also fixes the inner-page header). Keep B or D for later, if you want the home page
to feel more alive.

## 2. The fighter's 6.2 MB download (Divine Casualties)

### Where the weight is (measured)

| What | Size | Loaded when |
|---|---|---|
| Body-part cut-outs: 42 PNG files for 4 gods (11–13 each, about 150–220 × 290–380 pixels) | **5.5 MB** (Athena 1.6, Hades 1.9, Poseidon 1.0, Zeus 0.9) | as soon as the fighter opens, all four gods |
| Select-screen portraits (`heroes.jpg`) | 0.47 MB | when the fighter opens |
| Code and other files | about 0.2 MB | when the Symbols page loads |

### Options, measured by re-encoding every part in the browser

| Option | Download for the parts | Saving | Quality cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. **Convert the parts to WebP, same size** (quality 85) | **0.86 MB** | −84% | None visible. WebP keeps the transparent edges. Every current browser shows it (Safari since 2020). |
| 2. **Load only the two gods in the match** (after "To the arena") | about half of whatever format is used | about −50% | None. A short wait (well under a second on 4G after option 1) when the match starts, which the existing loading screen covers. |
| 3. **WebP at 60% size** (quality 80) | 0.43 MB | −92% | See below: depends on how big the parts are drawn |
| 4. **WebP at half size** (quality 85) | 0.37 MB | −93% | See below |
| 5. **Portraits (`heroes.jpg`, 1920×1280) as WebP** | 418 KB at full size; **216 KB at 60% size**; 171 KB at half | −11% / **−54%** / −64% | None at full size. 60% is probably invisible; half may look slightly soft on large high-density screens |

**How big the parts are actually drawn (measured during a match):** at most **0.47×** the
art's own resolution on a 1440-pixel high-density laptop, 0.26× on a 1920-pixel desktop
screen, and **0.20× on a phone**. The art is more than twice as detailed as any screen shows.
So **half-size art (option 4) costs nothing visible on any screen measured**, and is safe.

**Recommendation: options 1 + 2 + 4 + 5 together.** Half-size WebP parts, only the two gods in
the match, and the portrait sheet at 60% size as WebP. A match then downloads roughly **0.4 MB
instead of 6.2 MB**, about 94% less, with no visible change on today's screens. Effort is
**small to medium**:
- a script to convert the files (the PNG originals stay in `art-source/`);
- a change in `fighter.js` to load the WebP files, and to load the two chosen gods when the
  match starts instead of all four;
- updating the parts' manifests and re-running the fighter test.

**The one real cost:** if the fighter is ever shown much bigger than today (full screen on a
4K monitor), half-size art could start to look soft. The full-size originals stay in
`art-source/`, so that's reversible.

**What I'd skip:** cutting the number of parts, or flattening the gods into single images.
That would break the animation rig, which is what makes the fighter move well.
