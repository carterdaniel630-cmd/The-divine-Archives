## Design direction (locked character — no drift)
A single hero Pip in a friendly front-facing pose, simplified for hard-enamel: round
**pumpkin/onion-shaped** body, **moss-green** fill, **glossy golden** eyes, **leafy tuft**
on top, **chibi** proportions. Because enamel flattens the painterly style into color
fields, translate the storybook look into clean enamel color zones **without** losing the
locked silhouette — and never into the retired seed/root form.

## Product type
Hard-enamel lapel pin, ~1–1.5in, rubber clutch back.
