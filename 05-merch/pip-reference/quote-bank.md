## Card format (established, keep consistent)
Each piece pairs Pip (locked reference) with a quote in this layout:
- **Top:** the main line in bold caps, with **one keyword emphasized in an accent color**
  (e.g. blue, pink, purple, green, gold — matched to the scene).
- **Bottom:** a short sub-line (often with a **❤**).
- **Scene:** Pip in a warm **golden-hour** nature setting (hills, meadow, forest, river,
  blossom, snow, shore, desert — varied), painterly storybook style.
