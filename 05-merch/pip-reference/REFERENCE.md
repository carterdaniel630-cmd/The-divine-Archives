## The character (canonical)
- **Silhouette:** a rounded **onion / bulb** shape — slightly taller than wide, soft-tapering
  toward the crown, with a wider rounded base. **Chibi proportions:** big body/head, small
  stubby limbs.
- **Body color & texture:** warm **golden-brown / tan onion** skin with subtle **papery
  onion-skin layering** — faint vertical striations and soft translucent highlights. This is
  an **onion**, not a green vegetable. **Color is FINAL** (Carter, 2026-08-18).
- **Sprout:** a small **green leafy sprout** (2–3 tiny leaves) at the very top — the only
  green on Pip, reading as the onion's shoot.
- **Eyes:** very **large, round, glossy**, dark, with warm **amber-gold** reflections and
  bright catchlights. The most expressive feature.
- **Face:** small, with **rosy blushed cheeks** and a tiny gentle smile; no prominent nose.
- **Limbs:** short, stubby arms and legs; small rounded hands; **bare little feet**.
- **Style:** soft **painterly storybook** illustration — warm palette, gentle rim/back light,
  hand-illustrated feel.
- **Lighting & setting:** **golden hour**, outdoors — rolling hills, a path, grassy meadows,
  butterflies; low warm sun.
- **Voice / tone:** wholesome, gentle, **encouraging** — kindness and positivity. Captions
  are short affirmations.

## Retired / forbidden (reject on sight)
The old **seed-being / root-system** Pip — a germinating seed, trailing roots, a root-network
body, seedling/sapling forms, or tendrils in place of the green sprout. **Retired.** Any
drift toward it is rejected, not revised.

## ✅ Color — resolved, FINAL
Pip is a **brown/golden onion** (green only in the sprout). Carter confirmed 2026-08-18; the
earlier "moss-green" description is retired. Not an open question.
