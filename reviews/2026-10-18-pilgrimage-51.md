FIXABLE (for the builder; not fixed here, this run makes no commits)
  1. site:ararat, docs/pilgrimage/sites/mount-ararat-the-ark-tradition/site.js route (lines 83–97):
     the guided walk never reaches the summit for visitors with reduced motion on. The leg from the west
     end of the formation to the east end (2930,30 to 3095,0) is 167 m; the engine's reduced-motion jump
     stops after 80 m (engine.js line 335, 400 steps of 0.2 m), and "Walk on" then picks the nearest stop
     behind and walks back. It loops between two points for ever (60 legs tested). Split the long legs
     (also 3095,0 to 3000,95.8, 134 m) into stops under 80 m apart, or lift the cap in the engine.
  2. site:ararat, site.js line 113: "Its last known eruption came with the great earthquake of 1840"
     is stated as fact. The 2019 Ahora Gorge study the source log itself cites calls the event's causes
     "obscure and ambiguous", and other accounts doubt there was an eruption. Say "an eruption may have
     accompanied" and add the Contested flag.
  3. site:ararat, site.js line 131: "hold more than 60% of all the glacier ice in Turkey". The source
     (Azzoni, Sarıkaya and Fugazza 2020) says more than 60% of Turkey's glacier *area*. Say "area".
  4. site:ararat, site.js line 134: "Nothing on this summit has been found that is older than the
     climbers who reached it." is not in either cited source (Livius, Iranica), and with the next
     sentence it reads as a verdict on believers. Use a sourced, neutral line instead (for example
     that no find on the mountain has been verified as ancient).
  5. site:ararat, site.js line 113 and dims.js line 23: "about 35 km across" is marked SURVEY, but the
     logged source (Britannica) gives about 40 km; 35 km is Wikipedia's figure, which is not logged.
     Log the real source, or say "35–40 km".
  6. site:stonehenge, site.js lines 117 and 151, dims.js line 62, source log line 35: the Heel Stone
     "about 4.9 metres tall" is marked SURVEY from English Heritage, but I could not find 4.9 m in any
     English Heritage page or elsewhere; the usual figure is about 4.7 m above ground. Re-check, or correct.
  7. site:stonehenge, site.js lines 137 and 151, dims.js line 45: the Great Trilithon "stood 7.3 metres
     tall" is marked SURVEY (English Heritage). English Heritage's own figures found were lower for the
     standing stone (its teachers' kit implies about 6.5 m; its blog gives Stone 56 as 8.71 m with 2.13 m
     buried). 7.3 m appears in secondary sources and fits the height with its lintel. Say "with its
     lintel" and log the source that gives 7.3 m.
  8. site:golgotha, site.js line 133: "Christian tradition from the late 4th century says the cross was
     found at this site in Constantine's time". Cyril of Jerusalem already says so in his letter to
     Constantius (about 351); the late-4th-century date belongs to the Helena story (Ambrose, 395). Say
     "from the mid-4th century", or "the story of Helena's finding, from the late 4th century". This adds
     a source, so the builder (not the reviewer) should make it.
  9. site:pilgrimage-engine, docs/pilgrimage/engine.js lines 125–128: the new `anim` hook was inserted
     between the comment "a golden portal (portal.js) ..." and the `portal` line it describes, so that
     comment now sits above the wrong line. Cosmetic.
